Oman's e-invoicing programme (Fawtara), run by the Oman Tax Authority (OTA), covers both B2B and B2C transactions - unlike the UAE, where B2C remains out of scope. It's built on the same Peppol-based framework as the UAE (with Oman's own PINT-OM specification), using structured UBL XML and real-time reporting through an accredited service provider.

## Overview

**Rollout timeline:**

| Milestone | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Publication of Technical Specification | Nov 2025 |
| Service Provider Standards (ASP) | Dec 2025 |
| Developer / Test Portal Launch | Feb 2026 |
| Registration and Accreditation | May 2026 |
| First Phase of Implementation (selected taxpayers) | Aug 2026 |
| Completion of all Phases | Aug 2028 |

**What taxpayers should do next:**

- **System readiness** - identify every system that generates a sales invoice (POS, e-commerce, billing, ERP, manual), run a gap analysis against Oman's required data fields, and assess the changes needed.
- **Compliance** - understand the requirements that apply to your business, assess impact on current accounting practice, and confirm your go-live date under the phased rollout.
- **Team** - assign a team to own the implementation.

**Regulatory basis:** Fawtara is run by the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) on a five-corner Peppol model, covering both B2B and B2C transactions. Rollout is phased, beginning with an initial wave of larger taxpayers and extending over subsequent years to cover all taxpayers within scope. Source: [Oman Tax Authority - Fawtara](https://fawtara.taxoman.gov.om/)
