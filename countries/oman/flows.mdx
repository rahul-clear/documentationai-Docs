## Sales Side Flow

Oman's mandate covers B2B alongside B2C, reusing the same underlying reporting framework (accredited service provider, structured XML, real-time reporting).

<Expandable title="B2B" default-open="true">
As **Corner 2** (the seller's Access Point), ClearTax handles:

- Data validation and enrichment (including UUID generation)
- Validating the buyer's Peppol ID
- Delivery to the buyer's Access Point
- Tracking responses from the government and the buyer/buyer's Access Point
- Archival storage and retrieval
- Reconciliation and VAT filing summary preparation

<Expandable title="B2C" default-open="false">
If you run a VAT-registered business in Oman and sell to everyday customers, Fawtara applies to you: every consumer sale must be reported to the Tax Authority, and each customer must receive a clear receipt carrying a QR code.

**Documents involved:**

- **Tax Invoice** - the core electronic document reported for every sale. An ordinary consumer sale uses a simplified tax invoice; sales under the profit-margin scheme (e.g. used goods) use a full tax invoice under that scheme.
- **Credit notes** - issued to refund or correct a sale, referencing the original invoice, and reported the same way.
- **Receipt with QR code** - given to the customer as proof of purchase. It isn't submitted to the Tax Authority itself, but carries the QR code linking back to the reported invoice.

ClearTax supports two deployment modes for QR generation and reporting - both produce identical, OTA-compliant output:

- **Online (cloud)** - the seller's POS/ERP sends each invoice to ClearTax over API/SFTP; ClearTax generates the Seller UUID and QR, returns them for printing, and reports the invoice to OTA in the background. Best suited to modern ERP/e-commerce setups with reliable internet and enterprise-range volumes.
- **Offline (SDK)** - a ClearTax Java SDK deployed on the seller's POS terminals generates the QR and UUID locally with no network call; invoice XMLs queue locally and are submitted to OTA whenever connectivity is available. Best suited to high-volume, multi-store setups (50+ POS terminals, >1M invoices/month) or locations with unreliable connectivity - e.g. pharmacy chains, supermarkets, hypermarkets, fast-food chains, fuel retailers.

A single seller can mix both modes (e.g. SDK at remote stores, cloud API at head office or SDK to generate QR codes and online APIs for government reporting).

## Purchase Side Flow

As your registered Access Point (Corner 3), ClearTax handles the receiving side of the same 5-corner model used for sales:

- Invoices arriving from your suppliers' Access Points are accepted in the PINT-OM format and normalised into a common representation, made available on the platform.
- Inbound invoices are validated against PINT-OM schema and business rules before being made available for action.
- Your team is notified of new invoices, and can accept or reject them.
- Accepted invoices can be validated and reconciled against the corresponding purchase order.
- Accounting entries are made available for your ERP, along with archival storage and retrieval of all purchase documents.
