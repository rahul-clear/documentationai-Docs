Reports gives you an aggregated view of your e-invoicing activity - how many invoices have flowed through the system, their status distribution, day-over-day volume, and the underlying financial totals. Where the Sales Documents and Purchase Documents sections let you inspect individual documents, Reports lets you understand the shape of the whole book of business at a glance.

The current release ships with one report: the MIS Dashboard. Purchase-side reporting is on the roadmap.

## Getting to Reports

From the e-Invoicing app, click the Reports icon in the left sidebar. You'll land on the MIS Dashboard.

## The MIS Dashboard

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/product-guide/reports/mis-dashboard-on-sales-tab.png" width="2048" height="1078" alt="MIS Dashboard on Sales tab" />

The MIS Dashboard has five zones from top to bottom: tab switcher (Sales / Purchase), filter controls, KPI cards, charts, and the Overview table.

### Sales and Purchase tabs

Two tabs sit at the top of the dashboard:

- **Sales** - active by default. Covers your outbound documents ([Sales Documents](/countries/oman/product-guide/sales-documents), plus any self-billed invoices you've created).
- **Purchase** - currently disabled. Hovering shows a "Coming Soon" tooltip.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/product-guide/reports/purchase-tab-coming-soon-tooltip.png" width="2048" height="1085" alt="Purchase tab: Coming Soon tooltip" />

Purchase-side reporting will land in a subsequent release; until then, use the [Purchase Documents](/countries/oman/product-guide/purchase-documents) list view for inbound monitoring.

### Filter panel

Click Filter to open the filter side panel.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/product-guide/reports/mis-dashboard-filter-panel.png" width="2048" height="1081" alt="MIS Dashboard filter panel" />

The panel groups filters into four sections:

| Filter group    | Options                                                                                                 |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Document date range | Start and end date (DD/MM/YY format). Sets the window over which everything on the dashboard is calculated. |
| Document status     | Pending, Submitted to exchange network, Delivered to buyer network, Delivered to buyer, Failed              |
| Mode of generation  | Manual ingestion – Web, API, SFTP - filter by how documents entered ClearTax                                |
| Document type       | Tax invoice, Debit note, Credit note, Pre-payment invoice, Other                                            |

The "Other" bucket includes Commercial invoice, Self-billed invoice, Self-billed credit note, Invoice out of scope of tax, and Credit note related to goods or services. Selecting Other filters to any of these; use the [Sales Documents](/countries/oman/product-guide/sales-documents) list view with a specific Document Type filter if you need to isolate one type precisely.

Applied filters appear as chips above the KPI cards. Each chip has an × to clear it individually; Clear All removes every filter at once. Panel controls at the bottom: Apply Filter, Clear All, and × to close without applying.

The Mode of generation filter is particularly useful for validating your integration health - filtering to API shows only invoices that flowed via API, which helps ERP teams isolate their own submissions from manual ones.

### KPI cards

Four cards run across the top of the dashboard. Each has an info icon that shows the exact definition on hover - the definitions matter because the KPIs use slightly broader inclusion rules than the raw Sales Documents statuses.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/product-guide/reports/info-tooltip-on-invoices-delivered-kpi.png" width="2048" height="118" alt="Info tooltip on &quot;Invoices Delivered&quot; KPI" />

| KPI                                     | What it measures                                                                           | Tooltip content                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Invoices Ingested                           | Total documents that entered ClearTax in the selected window, regardless of downstream outcome | (no tooltip)                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Invoices Delivered                          | Count of documents that have completed delivery through the Peppol network                     | "Includes both Delivered to Buyer's service provider & Delivered to Buyer" - any invoice that has reached the buyer's Access Point (C3) counts as delivered, whether or not it has yet reached the buyer's ERP (C4). |
| Total Value of Invoices Delivered (OMR)     | Sum of gross document amounts for delivered invoices                                           | Same "Includes both…" note - value is summed across both status states                                                                                                                                               |
| Total Tax Value of Invoices Delivered (OMR) | Sum of VAT portions for delivered invoices                                                     | Same "Includes both…" note                                                                                                                                                                                           |

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/product-guide/reports/info-tooltip-on-total-value-kpi.png" width="2048" height="113" alt="Info tooltip on &quot;Total Value&quot; KPI" />

Why this matters: the "Delivered" KPI is a business view of delivery (the invoice is out of your hands and in the buyer's network), not the strictest technical view (which requires reaching the buyer's ERP). If you need only the strictest terminal state, use the Overview table's "Delivered to buyer" column instead of the KPI.

The gap between Invoices Ingested and Invoices Delivered is where operational attention needs to go - invoices ingested but not yet delivered are either in progress, pending, or failed.

### Status breakdown chart

A half-donut chart showing the distribution of documents across statuses within the selected window.

Key elements:

- **Centre label** - shows the count and percentage of the largest segment by default (e.g. 201 · 69.8% · Delivered to buyer)
- **Legend below** - lists every status with its share (colour-coded)
- **Segments** - each status is a wedge sized proportionally to its share

Hover over any segment to see its exact count.

### Daywise breakdown chart

A stacked bar chart showing daily document volume across the selected window.

- **X-axis** - dates (only days with activity are labelled; empty days are compressed)
- **Y-axis** - document count (0 to the visible maximum)
- **Bar segments** - same status colour coding as the Status breakdown donut, stacked within each day's bar

The chart is useful for spotting anomalies - a sudden spike in Failed on a particular day usually maps to an upstream ERP or integration issue.

### Overview table

Below the charts, the Overview table gives a per-business-name breakdown of counts and monetary values, cross-tabulated by status.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/product-guide/reports/overview-table-left-side.png" width="2048" height="258" alt="Overview table (left side)" />

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/product-guide/reports/overview-table-scrolled-right.png" width="2048" height="258" alt="Overview table (scrolled right)" />

Row grouping: each row is one entity + registration identifier combination under your workspace. The grouping key is:

- **Entity Name** - the legal name of the business
- **VAT** - Tax Registration Number
- **TIN** - Tax Identifier Number

If you operate a single entity, you'll see one row. If you operate multiple entities, or a single entity with multiple VAT/TIN combinations (branches, tax groups, etc.), each combination appears as its own row.

Column grouping: the columns are organised into blocks by status. Each status block has three sub-columns:

| Sub-column            | Meaning                       |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Invoice                   | Count of documents in this status |
| Total invoice value (OMR) | Sum of gross document amounts     |
| Total tax amount (OMR)    | Sum of VAT portions               |

Status blocks visible: Total, Pending, Submitted to exchange network, Delivered to buyer network, Delivered to buyer, Failed.

The table scrolls horizontally to reveal all status blocks; the leftmost columns (Business name and Total) remain visible as you scroll right.

## Using the MIS Dashboard in practice

A few common workflows the MIS Dashboard supports well:

**Daily health check.** Set the filter to the last 24–48 hours; scan the Status breakdown donut for any red (Failed) or larger-than-usual yellow (Pending) wedges. Drill into Sales Documents filtered by that status to remediate.

**Month-end close.** Set the filter to the calendar month; compare Invoices Ingested vs. Invoices Delivered to identify any documents that entered but didn't complete the flow. The Overview table's Failed and Pending columns show the gap in count and value.

**Integration monitoring.** Filter by Mode of generation = API to see only automated submissions; a sudden dip or a spike in Failed on API-mode invoices usually points to an ERP or middleware issue rather than a user error.

**Multi-entity oversight.** If your workspace covers multiple VATs, the Overview table's per-business rows let you compare volume and success rates across entities at a glance.
