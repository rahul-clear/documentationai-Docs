Purchase Documents is where you manage all inbound e-invoices your suppliers send you - tax invoices, credit notes, debit notes, and other documents delivered to you via the Peppol network. Unlike Sales, invoices here are received, not created: your supplier is the sender, ClearTax is your Access Point (C3 in the 5-corner model), and documents land in your Purchase Documents view once they arrive.

This section covers three activities:

1. Monitoring incoming documents as they arrive from the Peppol network
2. Reviewing individual documents in detail
3. Exporting documents to your AP workflow

## Getting to Purchase Documents

From the e-Invoicing dashboard, either click the Purchase documents tile in the main area, or click the Purchase Documents icon in the left sidebar. Both land you on the Purchase Documents list view.

## The Purchase Documents list view

Layout mirrors the Sales list - status tiles, actions bar, filter chips, document table - with two key differences: the status set is inbound-oriented, and the counterparty columns show Seller (your supplier) instead of Customer.

### Status summary tiles (top)

Six tiles at the top show inbound counts, scoped to your current filter selection:

| Tile                      | Meaning                                                        |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Total                         | All documents matching current filters                             |
| Received                      | Document has arrived in Purchase Documents from the Peppol network |
| Pending                       | (Reserved for self-billed invoices - see 4.8)                      |
| Failed                        | Processing or validation failed on receipt                         |
| Submitted to exchange network | (Reserved for self-billed invoices - see 4.8)                      |
| Delivered to seller network   | Reached your Access Point (C3)                                     |
| Delivered to seller           | Reached your ClearTax workspace                                    |

Terminology note: on the Purchase side, statuses use "seller" to describe you (you are the receiver of the invoice, but you are the seller in the ClearTax workspace context - the invoice is delivered to your workspace as the seller of the underlying goods/service being invoiced by the supplier). This mirrors the Peppol data model.

### Actions bar

Identical layout to Sales: Filter button (left); Selected Rows counter, Send button, and Actions dropdown (right).

### Active filter chips

Same as Sales - each applied filter appears as a chip with an × to clear it, plus a Clear All link.

### Document table

Default columns for Purchase Documents:

| Column                  | What it shows                                                                  |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Checkbox                    | Select the row for bulk actions                                                    |
| Document Number             | Supplier's invoice number (clickable - opens the detail view)                      |
| Document Status             | Where the document is                                                              |
| Government Reporting Status | OTA's view of the reporting outcome                                                |
| Document Date               | Invoice issue date                                                                 |
| Document Type               | Commercial invoice, Credit Note, Debit Note, Tax invoice, Prepayment Receipt, etc. |
| Seller Name                 | Your supplier's legal name                                                         |
| Seller VAT                  | Your supplier's Tax Registration Number                                            |

### Field-level view

Every section renders as a form with fields laid out in a four-column grid - same visual pattern as Sales. All fields on a Purchase document received from a supplier are read-only: no Edit mode is available. (Self-billed invoices you create are editable - see 4.8.)

## Understanding statuses

Every inbound document carries two independent status values.

### Document Status (Peppol → Your workspace)

| Status                    | What has happened                                                        | Applies to                                     |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Received                      | Document arrived from the Peppol network and is stored in Purchase Documents | Invoices received from suppliers (typical inbound) |
| Pending                       | Awaiting submission - queued or retrying                                     | Self-billed invoices you created (see 4.8)         |
| Failed                        | Submission or validation failed                                              | Self-billed invoices you created                   |
| Submitted to exchange network | Handed off to Peppol                                                         | Self-billed invoices you created                   |
| Delivered to seller network   | Reached the supplier's Access Point                                          | Self-billed invoices you created                   |
| Delivered to seller           | Reached the supplier's ClearTax workspace                                    | Self-billed invoices you created                   |

For received supplier invoices, the terminal state is Received + Accepted. For self-billed invoices you created, the terminal state is Delivered to seller + Accepted - mirroring the outbound Sales flow but running from your Purchase Documents view.

### Government Reporting Status (OTA)

Same set of values as Sales: Not Submitted, Processing, Accepted, Withdrawn. Runs in parallel with Document Status because OTA reporting is a separate flow.

### What "healthy" looks like

For a successfully received invoice, the terminal state is:

- **Document Status:** Received (or Delivered to seller)
- **Government Reporting Status:** Accepted

Anything else needs attention - Failed on Document Status typically indicates a schema or business-rule issue with what your supplier sent.

## Self-billed invoices

### What self-billed means

A self-billed invoice is one where the buyer creates the invoice on behalf of the seller. This inverts the usual role split: instead of the supplier issuing an invoice to the buyer, the buyer raises the invoice themselves and sends it to the supplier for confirmation.

Self-billing is common when:

- The buyer has better data (marketplaces, aggregators, franchise operators)
- The supplier is a small vendor who can't easily issue compliant invoices
- The contract terms make self-billing procedurally simpler

Whichever the reason, in Oman, the self-billed invoice is a valid tax document and flows through the Peppol network like any other invoice.

### Where self-billed invoices appear in ClearTax

Because you are the buyer creating the invoice, self-billed documents live in your Purchase Documents view - not Sales. This is why the Purchases side has actions and statuses that don't quite make sense for received supplier invoices: they exist to support the self-billed flow.

Correspondingly, the supplier on the other end sees your self-billed invoice arrive in their Sales Documents with status Received (see [Status summary tiles (top)](/countries/oman/product-guide/sales-documents)).

### Actions available on self-billed documents

Unlike received supplier invoices, self-billed invoices behave much like outbound Sales invoices:

| Capability                 | Received supplier invoice | Self-billed invoice you created  |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Edit                           | Not available (greyed out)    | Available                            |
| Send                           | Not applicable                | Available in Pending or Failed state |
| Save + Resubmit workflow       | Not applicable                | Available (same as outbound)         |
| Error banner + fix-to-continue | Not applicable                | Available                            |

### Status lifecycle for self-billed

Self-billed invoices you create move through the outbound-style status lifecycle:

Pending → Submitted to exchange network → Delivered to seller network → Delivered to seller

At any point, submission may enter Failed state - typically due to schema or business-rule validation issues on your supplier's data, missing mandatory fields, or OTA-side rejection. Fix the underlying issue (via Edit) and click Send to resubmit. Reading errors follows the same pattern as outbound (see [Troubleshooting](/countries/oman/product-guide/sales-documents)).

In parallel, the OTA Government Reporting Status transitions Not Submitted → Processing → Accepted (or Withdrawn).

### Filtering to see only self-billed documents

Use the Document Type filter and select the relevant self-billed types (Self-billed invoice, Self-billed credit note, etc.) to isolate them from the broader Purchase Documents list.

### Creating a self-billed invoice

See [Importing Documents](/countries/oman/product-guide/importing-documents) for the bulk upload flow, which supports self-billed invoices via a dedicated "Import Self-Billed Documents" option.

## Troubleshooting

Same pattern as Sales-side troubleshooting (see [Troubleshooting](/countries/oman/product-guide/sales-documents)). Two cases to distinguish:

- **Failed on a received supplier invoice** - indicates a receipt-time processing failure. Typically a schema or business-rule issue on your supplier's data. You cannot fix it directly (the document is theirs); coordinate with your supplier to resend a corrected version.
- **Failed on a self-billed invoice you created** - behaves exactly like a Failed outbound invoice. Open the detail view, read the error banner, fix the issue via Edit, and resubmit with Send.
