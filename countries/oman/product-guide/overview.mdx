This product guide walks through the ClearTax Oman E-Invoicing product end to end - from creating your workspace, through day-to-day use of the dashboard, Sales and Purchase Documents, bulk imports, reporting, workspace settings, and the built-in support desk.

Oman's e-invoicing programme, **Fawtara**, is run by the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) on a five-corner Peppol model. ClearTax exchanges your invoices over the Peppol network and reports them to the OTA, so you can issue and receive e-invoices directly from the ClearTax dashboard.

| Detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Tax authority | OTA (Oman Tax Authority) |
| E-invoicing programme | Fawtara |
| Exchange model | Five-corner Peppol model |
| Applies to | B2B and B2C invoices |
