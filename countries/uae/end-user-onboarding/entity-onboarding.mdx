---
title: Entity Onboarding
description: 'Process and requirements for adding a new entity (TIN) to an existing ClearTax workspace'
---
This scenario covers an **existing ClearTax customer** who already has an active workspace and needs to onboard an **additional legal entity (TRN/TIN)** under it - for example, a new branch, subsidiary, or sister company. It differs from the flows in [Onboarding via EmaraTax](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/onboarding-via-emaratax) and [Onboarding via ClearTax](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/onboarding-via-cleartax), which cover a taxpayer setting up ClearTax for the very first time, and from [Onboarding for Partners](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/onboarding-for-partners), where a partner adds the TIN on behalf of an end customer. Here, the account owner performs both the TIN addition and the EmaraTax linking themselves, end-to-end, without creating a new ClearTax account.

## Requirements

Before starting, make sure you have:

- An existing, active ClearTax workspace (admin or business-settings access)
- The new entity's **Business Identifier (TIN/TRN)**, **Business Name**, and **VAT Registration Type**
- Admin login credentials for the new entity on the **EmaraTax** portal, with access to select an ASP for that TIN

## Step-by-step process

### Step 1: Add the new TIN in Business settings

Log in to your existing ClearTax workspace and open **Settings > Business settings**. This page lists all the businesses (TRNs) already onboarded under the workspace.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/settings/business-settings-populated.png" width="1400" height="763" alt="Business settings - view and manage businesses" />

Click **Add business** to open the **Add new TRNs** modal and enter the Business Identifier (TIN), Business Name, and VAT Registration Type for the new entity.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/settings/add-trn-modal.png" width="1400" height="766" alt="Add new TRNs modal - enter Business Identifier, Business Name, and VAT Registration Type" />

The new TIN now shows as **Not Linked** under the Emaratax Status column until the EmaraTax linking below is completed.

### Step 2: Log in to EmaraTax

Log in to the EmaraTax portal of FTA-UAE with the admin credentials for the new entity, entering credentials and captcha.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/emaratax-login.jpg" width="1400" height="696" alt="EmaraTax login screen" />

### Step 3: Select e-Invoicing

Land on the EmaraTax homepage and choose e-Invoicing to begin the ASP linking process.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/emaratax-e-invoicing-menu.jpg" width="1400" height="698" alt="EmaraTax homepage with e-Invoicing option" />

### Step 4: Click on Proceed

EmaraTax shows the details of the actions possible on the e-Invoicing page. Click "Proceed" to move to the actual page.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/action-required-modal.jpg" width="1400" height="698" alt="EmaraTax e-Invoicing page Proceed action" />

### Step 5: Select the new TIN

Select the same TIN that was added in Business settings in Step 1, from the dropdown list of all TINs mapped in the EmaraTax account.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/select-tin.jpg" width="1400" height="700" alt="TIN selection dropdown on EmaraTax" />

### Step 6: Select "Defmacro" as ASP

After selecting the TIN, navigate to the page where ClearTax appears, listed under its legal entity name "Defmacro" - this is expected. Select the radio button against "Defmacro" and click "Proceed to ASP".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/select-defmacro-asp.jpg" width="1400" height="701" alt="Selecting Defmacro (ClearTax) as ASP on EmaraTax" />

### Step 7: Confirm redirection to ClearTax

A popup message appears on EmaraTax indicating you will be redirected to the ASP website. Click "Yes" to continue on ClearTax.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/confirm-redirect.jpg" width="1400" height="701" alt="Redirect confirmation popup on EmaraTax" />

### Step 8: Linking request on ClearTax

You are redirected to ClearTax, which opens the "Linking request" page. Enter the registered EmaraTax email address, TIN number, and phone number (optional), then click "Verify applicant details".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/linking-request-form.jpg" width="1400" height="875" alt="ClearTax Linking request page" />

### Step 9: Confirm details

Review the taxpayer information fetched from EmaraTax, select the declaration checkbox, and click "Submit".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/confirm-details-unchecked.jpg" width="1400" height="701" alt="Taxpayer details fetched from EmaraTax" />

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/confirm-details-checked.jpg" width="1400" height="691" alt="Declaration checkbox and Submit on ClearTax" />

### Step 10: Complete verification and Peppol ID creation

EmaraTax verifies the taxpayer information and generates a Peppol participant ID, registered in the FTA's central directory. A success message is displayed with the option to click "Get Started".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/peppol-id-created.jpg" width="1400" height="697" alt="Verification success and Peppol ID creation" />

### Step 11: New entity available in the workspace

Back in ClearTax's Business settings, the new TIN's Emaratax Status now shows as **Linked**. The entity is available for selection alongside your other businesses, and you can begin generating and exchanging e-invoices under it right away - no new account or workspace is required.
