This section covers scenarios of a taxpayer in the UAE onboarding ClearTax as an accredited service provider (ASP) of e-invoicing on behalf of EmaraTax. It covers the two entry points into the onboarding flow, the possible error scenarios, and how they are handled so the user can clearly understand the error reason and navigate through successfully.

## Onboarding entry points

| Flow | Starting point |
| --- | --- |
| [Onboarding via EmaraTax](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/onboarding-via-emaratax) | Taxpayer admin begins on the EmaraTax portal and is redirected to ClearTax to complete setup |
| [Onboarding via ClearTax](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/onboarding-via-cleartax) | Taxpayer admin begins on ClearTax and is redirected to EmaraTax to link the TRN before returning to ClearTax |
| [Onboarding for Partners](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/onboarding-for-partners) | Partner adds the end customer's TIN in Business settings, then the end customer completes the EmaraTax linking and reports back to the partner instead of signing up on ClearTax |
| [New Entity Onboarding](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/entity-onboarding) | Existing ClearTax customer adds a new entity (TIN/TRN) to their workspace and completes the EmaraTax linking themselves |

## Understand your tax registration type

Before onboarding, identify which of the three UAE tax registration types applies to each entity you are onboarding. This determines the **VAT Registration Type** you select and what you enter in the **TRN** field on ClearTax.

| Registration type | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **VAT Group** | The company has a Group TRN, and each child entity operates under its own separate TIN. With Peppol document exchange, these TINs are used to receive invoices. |
| **VAT Registered** | The company has only a single TRN/TIN pair. |
| **Not VAT Registered** | The company has only a single TIN. |

A large conglomerate can have a combination of these registration types across its entities, so identify the type for each entity separately.

### What to enter on ClearTax

The VAT Registration Type is selected on two screens:

- The **Add new TRNs** modal in **Settings > Business settings**, using the **VAT Registration Type** dropdown
- The **Create Business Profile** step after selecting e-Invoicing, using the **VAT Registration Type** radio buttons (**Single VAT**, **Group VAT**, **Not registered**)

| Your registration type | Select | TRN field | Business / Organisation Name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VAT Group | Group VAT | Your actual Group TRN | Your business name |
| VAT Registered | Single VAT | Your actual TRN | Your business name |
| Not VAT Registered | Not registered | A free-text name, such as your entity or business name, instead of a TRN | Your business name - this can be the same as the free text entered in the TRN field |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
If you are **not VAT registered**, select the same **Not registered** option on both screens. The TRN field accepts free text in this case, and no validations are applied to the details entered.
</Callout>

See [Error Scenarios](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/error-scenarios) for validation failures that can occur during either flow.
