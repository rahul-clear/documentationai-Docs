---
title: Error Scenarios
description: 'Validation errors a UAE taxpayer may encounter during ClearTax onboarding'
---
The following validation errors can occur during either the EmaraTax-initiated or ClearTax-initiated onboarding flow:

1. **Email is wrong** - Email is validated for pattern, and any incorrect email will be flagged with a wrong email ID error message.
2. **TIN is wrong** - The TIN entered is not ten digits, or is not all numeric.
