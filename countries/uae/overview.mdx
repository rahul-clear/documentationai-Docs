The UAE will mandate e-invoicing for B2B and B2G transactions starting **July 2026**, adopting the DCTCE (5-corner) model over the Peppol network, driven by the Ministry of Finance. B2C remains out of scope.&#x20;

## Rollout timeline

| Date         | Milestone                                                                                        |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Jul 2023     | Ministry of Finance reveals the roadmap for B2B e-invoicing                                      |
| Feb 2025     | Certification requirements and the UAE data dictionary published                                 |
| Aug/Sep 2025 | E-invoicing legislation released; voluntary adoption begins                                      |
| Dec 2025     | Phased rollout strategy announced, based on taxpayer turnover                                    |
| Jul 2026     | Phase 1 implementation begins - mandatory go-live for the first taxpayer group (600k+ taxpayers) |

## Regulatory basis

Federal Decree-Law No. 16 of 2024 (amending the VAT Law) recognises e-invoices as valid tax documents, with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) as the competent authority. Businesses must select an Accredited Service Provider by 30 Oct 2026; mandatory go-live follows from 1 Jan 2027 for taxpayers with annual revenue of AED 50 million or more, and from 1 Jul 2027 for all remaining taxpayers. Invoices use the PINT AE data model.

Source: [FTA - e-Invoicing](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/uae.einvoicing.aspx)
