The UAE will mandate e-invoicing for B2B and B2G transactions starting **July 2026**, adopting the DCTCE (5-corner) model over the Peppol network, driven by the Ministry of Finance. B2C remains out of scope. ClearTax is aligned with the FTA and in the process of Service Provider Accreditation.

This page covers how sales and purchase invoices flow through ClearTax (see [Flows](/countries/uae/flows)), the invoice status lifecycle, and API reference material (see [Setup & Reference](/countries/uae/setup-reference)).

## Rollout timeline

| Date | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 11 Jul 2023 | Ministry of Finance reveals the roadmap for B2B e-invoicing |
| Feb 2025 | Certification requirements and the UAE data dictionary published |
| Aug/Sep 2025 | E-invoicing legislation released; voluntary adoption begins |
| Dec 2025 | Phased rollout strategy announced, based on taxpayer turnover |
| Jul 2026 | Phase 1 implementation begins - mandatory go-live for the first taxpayer group (600k+ taxpayers) |

## What changes beyond e-invoicing itself

Stricter output VAT filing compliance, pre-population of VAT returns directly from e-invoices, supplier accountability on e-invoicing affecting input tax claimability, and increased regulatory scrutiny on return filing and compliance overall.

## Regulatory basis

Federal Decree-Law No. 16 of 2024 (amending the VAT Law) recognises e-invoices as valid tax documents, with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA) as the competent authority. Businesses must select an Accredited Service Provider by 30 Oct 2026; mandatory go-live follows from 1 Jan 2027 for taxpayers with annual revenue of AED 50 million or more, and from 1 Jul 2027 for all remaining taxpayers. Invoices use the PINT AE data model.

Source: [FTA - e-Invoicing](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/uae.einvoicing.aspx)
