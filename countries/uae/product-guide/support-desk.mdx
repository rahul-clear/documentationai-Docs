Support Desk is where you raise and track tickets with the ClearTax support team without leaving the product. It's built into the top bar of every screen inside the e-Invoicing app, so you can flag an issue from wherever you are - a stuck submission, a validation error you don't understand, a missing feature - and follow it through to resolution.

Two capabilities:

- Raise ticket - describe your issue, attach a screenshot, and submit
- All tickets - view the status of everything you've raised

## Getting to Support Desk

Click Support Desk in the top bar (top-right of the e-Invoicing app, between the language toggle and the notifications bell).

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/support-desk/support-desk-tooltip.jpg" width="1400" height="797" alt="Support Desk button with tooltip" />

Clicking opens the Support Desk side panel.

## The Support Desk panel

Two cards:

| Card | Purpose | Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raise ticket | Opens the ticket-creation form to describe a new issue. "We will collect details to report it & will get to work." | Ctrl + Q |
| All tickets | Opens the list of tickets you've raised, with their current status. "Track the status of the active & closed tickets." | - |

Pick one to continue.

## Raising a ticket

Click Raise ticket (or press Ctrl + Q) to open the ticket form.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/support-desk/raise-ticket-form.jpg" width="1400" height="798" alt="Raise a ticket form" />

### Screenshot capture

The form opens with a screenshot capture zone at the top - this is the fastest way to give support the context they need.

- **Auto-captured on open:** launching the form via Ctrl + Q or clicking Support Desk in the top bar automatically captures the screen you were looking at. The captured screenshot appears in the zone, ready to submit with the ticket.
- **Capture screenshot** button: if no screenshot is attached, click this to take one manually.
- **Add more attachments:** attach additional screenshots, error files, or supporting documents (invoice XML, error report exports, etc.).

### Issue subject

**Required.** A short one-line summary of the issue.

### Issue description

**Required.** Full details of what went wrong: what you were doing, what you expected, what actually happened, and any error messages you saw.

### Call-back contact number

Optional. If the issue is urgent or needs a phone conversation to unblock, add a callback number. Country code defaults to +971; switch via the dropdown if needed.

### Additional contacts in CC

Optional. Comma-separated email addresses to CC on ticket updates.

### Submitting the ticket

Two buttons at the bottom:

- **Cancel** - closes the form without saving
- **Raise ticket** - submits the ticket to the ClearTax support team

Once submitted, the ticket appears in All tickets with an initial status. You'll receive updates via email to the address on your account (and any CC contacts you added).

Behind the scenes, the ClearTax team categorises the ticket internally and routes it to the right specialist - you don't need to pick a category or priority when raising it.

## Tracking tickets

Click All tickets from the Support Desk panel to see everything you've raised.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/support-desk/all-tickets-empty.jpg" width="1400" height="797" alt="All Tickets empty state" />

### Empty state

If you haven't raised anything recently, you'll see the empty state:

> "No tickets to show. You've not raised any tickets in the last 90 days. For older requests, please contact our support team."

### The 90-day window

All tickets show only tickets raised in the last 90 days. For anything older, contact the ClearTax support team directly.
