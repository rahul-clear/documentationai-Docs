title: Onboarding
description: 'Creating a ClearTax account and linking your TRN via EmaraTax to register ClearTax as your Accredited Service Provider'
---
Onboarding sets up your workspace on ClearTax and links it to the FTA's EmaraTax system so that ClearTax can act as your Accredited Service Provider (ASP) for e-invoicing. Once onboarding is complete, your Peppol Participant ID is registered with the FTA and you can begin exchanging invoices from the ClearTax dashboard.

The flow starts on ClearTax, briefly redirects you to EmaraTax to select ClearTax as your ASP, and returns you to ClearTax to complete linking and land on your dashboard. As an alternative you can start on the EmaraTax portal instead - see [End User Onboarding](/countries/uae/end-user-onboarding/overview) for that entry point and the possible error scenarios.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- An active EmaraTax account with admin access
- One or more TRN\<\>TIN pairs mapped to that EmaraTax account
- The admin email address registered on EmaraTax - this is the email against which ClearTax will verify and map your account, so use the same one on both sides
- Access to the ClearTax UAE URL: [https://app.mea.cleartax.com/new-einvoicing](https://app.mea.cleartax.com/new-einvoicing)

## Create your ClearTax account

### Step 1 - Sign up

Go to [https://app.mea.cleartax.com/new-einvoicing](https://app.mea.cleartax.com/new-einvoicing). Enter the email address you use on EmaraTax (this is mandatory - verification only works against that email) and click Continue.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/sign-up.jpg" width="1400" height="699" alt="Create your account page" />

### Step 2 - Set a password and accept terms

Enter and confirm your password, tick the T&Cs checkbox, and click Agree and continue.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/password-setup.jpg" width="1400" height="697" alt="Password creation with T&Cs" />

### Step 3 - Set up your workspace

Enter your company's legal name and select the applicable turnover band (< 25mn AED, 25mn - 100mn AED, > 100mn AED). Click Continue.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/workspace-setup.jpg" width="1400" height="700" alt="Let's create your workspace" />

### Step 4 - Personalise your profile

Enter your name and select your designation (CFO/Director, Head/VP/GM, Manager, or Other). Click Continue.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/personalise-profile.jpg" width="1400" height="700" alt="Help us personalize your experience" />

### Step 5 - Open the e-Invoicing app

You'll see a "Welcome to Clear" popup. Click Continue, then click the e-Invoicing card on the Clear Compliance Cloud landing page.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/welcome-popup-1.jpg" width="1400" height="698" alt="Welcome to Clear popup" />

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/welcome-popup-2.jpg" width="1400" height="701" alt="Clear Compliance Cloud landing with e-Invoicing card" />

## Link your TRN via EmaraTax

### Step 6 - Welcome to E-invoicing

After you click the e-Invoicing card, you'll land on the "Say hello to E-invoicing" welcome screen - the first step of the multi-country onboarding wizard shown in the left sidebar (Welcome &rarr; Add Country &rarr; Create Business Profile).

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/welcome-e-invoicing.jpg" width="1400" height="797" alt="Step 1: Welcome - multi-country onboarding wizard" />

### Step 7 - Select your first country and trigger EmaraTax

On the "Select your first country" step, choose UAE. (Jordan and Oman are also visible if you plan to onboard there later; you can add more countries from the Business Settings page once you've finished setting up your first.) A warning banner appears: "Couldn't find a TRN associated with this account, you have not completed registration on EmaraTax. Please come back here after finishing registration on EmaraTax." Click Go to EmaraTax.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/select-country.jpg" width="1400" height="798" alt="Step 2: Add Country - country selection with TRN warning" />

### Step 8 - Log in to EmaraTax

Enter your credentials and captcha at the EmaraTax portal.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/emaratax-login.jpg" width="1400" height="696" alt="EmaraTax login screen" />

### Step 9 - Open the e-Invoicing menu

From the EmaraTax homepage left sidebar, click E-INVOICING.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/emaratax-e-invoicing-menu.jpg" width="1400" height="698" alt="EmaraTax home with E-INVOICING menu highlighted" />

### Step 10 - Review the e-Invoicing Action Required prompt

EmaraTax explains the available actions (onboarding, reverification, offboarding). Click Proceed to continue.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/action-required-modal.jpg" width="1400" height="698" alt="e-Invoicing Action Required modal" />

### Step 11 - Select the TIN you want to onboard

From the dropdown, choose the TIN under which you'll be issuing e-invoices.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/select-tin.jpg" width="1400" height="700" alt="TIN dropdown" />

### Step 12 - Select "Defmacro" as your ASP

From the ASP list, navigate to the page where Defmacro appears (ClearTax is listed on EmaraTax under its legal entity name, Defmacro - this is expected). Select the radio button next to it and click Proceed to ASP.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/select-defmacro-asp.jpg" width="1400" height="701" alt="ASP list with Defmacro row selected" />

### Step 13 - Confirm the redirection back to ClearTax

A confirmation popup appears warning that you're about to leave EmaraTax. Click Yes.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/confirm-redirect.jpg" width="1400" height="701" alt="Leave EmaraTax confirmation" />

## Complete the linking request

### Step 14 - Enter applicant details

You'll return to ClearTax on the Linking request page. Enter:

- Registered EmaraTax email address
- TIN (Tax Identifier Number)
- Mobile number (optional; country code defaults to +971)

Click Verify applicant details.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/linking-request-form.jpg" width="1400" height="875" alt="Linking request form" />

### Step 15 - Confirm your prefilled details

ClearTax fetches your taxpayer information from EmaraTax and displays it read-only: Legal Type, Entity Name (English and Arabic), VAT TRN, effective registration date, and submission date. Review the details, tick the declaration checkbox, and click Submit.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/confirm-details-unchecked.jpg" width="1400" height="701" alt="Confirmation of details, checkbox unchecked" />

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/confirm-details-checked.jpg" width="1400" height="691" alt="Same page with checkbox ticked and Submit active" />

### Step 16 - Peppol ID creation

EmaraTax verifies the submission and generates your Peppol Participant ID (format: `0235:<TIN>`), which is registered in the FTA's central Peppol directory. On success, you'll see the "All done!" screen with four confirmation checkpoints:

- Verification with EmaraTax
- PEPPOL ID created and registered
- PEPPOL ID updated in FTA directory
- ASP onboarding completed

Click Get Started.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/peppol-id-created.jpg" width="1400" height="697" alt="All done! success screen" />

## Land on your dashboard

### Step 17 - Confirm the welcome screen

You'll return to the welcome screen, this time without the warning banner. Click Continue.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/welcome-clean.jpg" width="1400" height="697" alt="Welcome page (clean state)" />

### Step 18 - Confirm your business profile

You'll land on the Business profile page - Step 3 of the onboarding wizard ("Create Business Profile"). The form asks for:

- **Organisation Identifier (TRN or Group TRN)** - your VAT registration number
- **Organisation Name** - your legal entity name

If EmaraTax linking has completed successfully, both fields are prefilled from EmaraTax and cannot be edited - they must exactly match your FTA registration. If you've arrived here before linking is complete, the fields are empty. Click Continue to Dashboard.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/business-profile.jpg" width="1400" height="698" alt="Business profile with prefilled fields (post-linking)" />

### Step 19 - Your ClearTax dashboard

You'll now land on the e-Invoicing dashboard. From here you can:

- View **Sales documents** and **Purchase documents** overviews (populating as invoices flow in)
- Access **Business insights and analytics** - MIS Dashboard
- Run **Reconciliation** - Sales Register vs. e-Invoice, and General Ledger vs. Sales e-Invoice
- Get help via **Product guides and videos**, and stay current with **Product release notes**
- Start exchanging invoices via bulk upload or API/SFTP integration (see [Importing Documents](/countries/uae/product-guide/importing-documents))

The TRN you onboarded is shown in the top bar - click it to switch entities if you manage multiple TRNs.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/product-guide/onboarding/dashboard.jpg" width="1400" height="699" alt="E-Invoicing dashboard" />
