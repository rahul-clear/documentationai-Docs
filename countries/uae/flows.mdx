## Sales Side Flow

<Expandable title="B2B" default-open="true">
  As **Corner 2** (the seller's Access Point), ClearTax handles:\


  - Data validation and enrichment (including UUID generation)
  - Validation of the buyer's Peppol ID
  - Delivery to the buyer's Access Point
  - Tracking of responses from the government and the buyer's Access Point
  - Archival storage and retrieval
  - Reconciliation and VAT filing summary preparation
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="B2C" default-open="false">
  <Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
    B2C is out of scope for the UAE's current e-invoicing mandate - only B2B and B2G transactions are covered.
  </Callout>
</Expandable>

## Purchase Side Flow

<Expandable title="B2B" default-open="true">
  As **Corner 3** (the buyer's Access Point), ClearTax handles:\


  - Normalisation of inbound invoices from PINT-AE into clear representation on the platform
  - Validation of inbound invoices against PINT-AE schema and business rules
  - Notification of new invoices, with acceptance or rejection by your team
  - Tracking of responses from the government and the seller's Access Point
  - Accounting entries made available to your ERP
  - Archival storage and retrieval
</Expandable>
