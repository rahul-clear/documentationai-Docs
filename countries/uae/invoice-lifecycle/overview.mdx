## 5 Corner Peppol Exchange Network

The UAE has adopted the 5 Corner Peppol model for exchanging invoices. The network is used for:

- Exchanging invoices between Seller and Buyer
- Reporting invoices to the FTA

The diagram below shows the invoice exchange process and the role of each stakeholder:

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/uae/invoice-lifecycle/five-corner-peppol-model.png" width="1654" height="650" alt="5 Corner Peppol exchange network for the UAE" />

The 5 corners are:

- **C1** - The Seller (Buyer in case of Self-Billing)
- **C2** - C1's Access Point
- **C3** - C4's Access Point
- **C4** - The Buyer (Seller in case of Self-Billing)
- **C5** - FTA

Reporting to the FTA happens in the form of a **Tax Data Document (TDD)**, which is a subset of the fields of the invoice.

ClearTax can act as either access point, or both, depending on the taxpayers' selection of ASPs.

## Document and Government Reporting Status

The ClearTax e-invoicing product shows two statuses for every invoice:

- **Document Status** - Tracks invoice exchange between sender and recipient
- **Government Reporting Status** - Tracks reporting of the invoice data (Tax Data Document) to the FTA

### Document Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The invoice has not been sent to C2 |
| **Submitted to Exchange Network** | The invoice has been received by C2 |
| **Delivered to Buyer Network** | The invoice has been received by C3 |
| **Delivered to Buyer** | The invoice has been received by C4 |
| **Failed** | The invoice delivery to the buyer has failed |

An invoice can move to **Failed** for any of the following reasons:

- Syntax/Business rule validation failed at C2
- C3 is down for an extended period, so automatic retries are exhausted
- Syntax/Business rule validation failed at C3
- C4 is permanently not reachable

### Government Reporting Status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not Submitted** | The TDD has not been sent to C5 |
| **Processing** | The TDD has been received by C5 |
| **Accepted** | The TDD has been validated successfully by C5 |
| **Withdrawn** | The TDD has been withdrawn from C5's records due to failure of invoice exchange |

See [Status Lifecycle](/countries/uae/invoice-lifecycle/status-lifecycle) for how these two statuses change together in each scenario.
