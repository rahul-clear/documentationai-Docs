The tables below show how invoice statuses change in the product in each case. For what each corner (C1-C5) and each status means, see the [Invoice Lifecycle Overview](/countries/uae/invoice-lifecycle/overview).

## Happy Case

| S.No | Event | Document Status | Government Reporting Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invoice not sent to C2 | Pending | Not Submitted |
| 2 | Invoice Received by C2 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 3 | Invoice Validated by C2 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 4 | TDD Created | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 5 | Invoice Sent by C2 to C3 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 6 | Invoice Received by C3 | Delivered to Buyer Network | Not Submitted |
| 7 | Invoice Validated by C3 | Delivered to Buyer Network | Not Submitted |
| 8 | TDD Received by C5 | Delivered to Buyer Network | Processing |
| 9 | Invoice Received by C4 | Delivered to Buyer | Processing |
| 10 | TDD Validated by C5 | Delivered to Buyer | Accepted |

## Invoice Validation Failed at C2

| S.No | Event | Document Status | Government Reporting Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invoice not sent to C2 | Pending | Not Submitted |
| 2 | Invoice Received by C2 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 3 | Invoice Validation Failed by C2 | Failed | Not Submitted |

**Next steps for the user/taxpayer:**

- Check the failure reason, modify the invoice accordingly and attempt again.

## Invoice Validation Failed at C3

Invoices can fail validation at C3 in the following cases:

- Discrepancies between validation rules at C2 and C3 due to updates from the FTA
- Invoices sent to C3 get corrupted in transit

| S.No | Event | Document Status | Government Reporting Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invoice not sent to C2 | Pending | Not Submitted |
| 2 | Invoice Received by C2 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 3 | Invoice Validated by C2 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 4 | Invoice Sent by C2 to C3 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 5 | TDD Created | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 6 | Invoice Received by C3 | Delivered to Buyer Network | Not Submitted |
| 7 | Invoice Validation Failed by C3 | Failed | Not Submitted |
| 8 | TDD Received by C5 | Failed | Processing |
| 9 | TDD Validated by C5 | Failed | Accepted |
| 10 | TDD Withdrawn from C5 | Failed | Withdrawn |

**Next steps for the user/taxpayer:**

- Check the failure reason, modify the invoice accordingly and attempt again.

## Invoice Validation Failed at C5

Invoices can fail validation at C5 in the following cases:

- ClearTax makes a mistake in extracting the TDD from the invoice data
- Invoices sent to C5 get corrupted in transit

| S.No | Event | Document Status | Government Reporting Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invoice not sent to C2 | Pending | Not Submitted |
| 2 | Invoice Received by C2 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 3 | Invoice Validated by C2 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 4 | Invoice Sent by C2 to C3 | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 5 | TDD Created | Submitted to Exchange Network | Not Submitted |
| 6 | Invoice Received by C3 | Delivered to Buyer Network | Not Submitted |
| 7 | Invoice Validated by C3 | Delivered to Buyer Network | Not Submitted |
| 8 | TDD Received by C5 | Delivered to Buyer Network | Processing |
| 9 | Invoice Received by C4 | Delivered to Buyer | Processing |
| 10 | TDD Validation Failed by C5 (ClearTax retries after fixing the TDD rejection reason) | Delivered to Buyer | Processing |

**Next steps for the user/taxpayer:**

- No action is needed from the user. ClearTax will resolve the TDD error and retry submission once the rejection reason is fixed. The SLA for resolution is 24 hours (this does not violate the 15-minute invoice SLA).
- If the issue is not resolved within 24 hours, automated jobs run by ClearTax will retry sending the invoice at a set frequency until the invoice is accepted.
