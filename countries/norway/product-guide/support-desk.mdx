Raise and track tickets with ClearTax support without leaving the product: a stuck submission, a Peppol delivery issue, a validation error you can't resolve, or a feature request.

## Getting to Support Desk

Click **Support Desk** in the top bar, between the language toggle and the notifications bell.

## The Support Desk panel

| Card         | Purpose                              |
|--------------|--------------------------------------|
| Raise ticket | Opens the ticket form                |
| All tickets  | Lists your active and closed tickets |

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/support-desk/support-desk-panel.png" width="2048" height="1149" alt="Support Desk panel" />

## Raising a ticket

- **Screenshot capture:** a screenshot is captured when the form opens. Use **Capture screenshot** or **Add more attachments** (invoice XML, error reports) as needed.
- **Issue subject** (required): a one-line summary.
- **Issue description** (required): what you did, what you expected, what happened, and any error messages.
- **Call-back contact number** (optional).
- **Additional contacts in CC** (optional): comma-separated emails.

Click **Raise ticket** to submit. Updates come by email. ClearTax categorises and routes the ticket internally, so you don't need to pick a category or priority.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/support-desk/raise-a-ticket-form.png" width="2048" height="1149" alt="Raise a ticket form" />

## Tracking tickets

**All tickets** shows tickets raised in the last 90 days. For older requests, contact the ClearTax support team directly.
