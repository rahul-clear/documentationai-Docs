The MIS Dashboard gives you a business-intelligence view of your e-invoicing activity for both sales and purchase documents.

## Accessing the dashboard

- Click **Reports** in the left navigation and select **MIS Dashboard for E-Invoicing**, or
- Click the **MIS Dashboard** card under *Business insights and analytics* on the home page.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/mis-dashboard/reports-mis-dashboard-for-e-invoicing.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Reports: MIS Dashboard for E-Invoicing" />

Use the **Sales** and **Purchase** tabs at the top to switch views.

## Filters

Click **Filter** to set:

- **Document date range** (defaults to the last 30 days)
- **Document status:** Pending, Submitted to exchange network, Delivered to buyer network, Delivered to buyer, Failed
- **Mode of generation:** Manual ingestion - Web, API, SFTP
- **Document type:** Tax invoice, Debit note, Credit note, Pre-payment invoice and more

Click **Apply Filter** to refresh the dashboard, or **Clear All** to reset.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/mis-dashboard/mis-filters.png" width="2048" height="1131" alt="MIS filters" />

## Summary metrics

| Metric                                      | Description                                  |
|---------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Invoices Ingested                           | Documents imported in the selected period    |
| Invoices Delivered                          | Documents delivered to the buyer             |
| Total Value of Invoices Delivered (NOK)     | Sum of invoice value for delivered documents |
| Total Tax Value of Invoices Delivered (NOK) | Sum of VAT for delivered documents           |

## Status breakdown and daywise breakdown

- **Status breakdown:** a gauge showing the share of documents in each status (for example Delivered to buyer, Failed, Pending, Submitted to exchange network, Delivered to buyer network).
- **Daywise breakdown:** a stacked bar chart of document counts per day, split by status.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/mis-dashboard/status-and-daywise-breakdown.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Status and daywise breakdown" />

## Overview table

The overview table shows, for each business (VAT) and branch, the invoice count, total invoice value (NOK) and total tax amount (NOK), split by status: Total, Pending, Submitted to exchange network, Delivered to buyer network and onwards.
