Onboarding has three parts: create your ClearTax profile, register your Norwegian business, and get your Peppol ID registered.

## Create your profile

**Step 1: Sign up**

- Go to the ClearTax e-Invoicing portal: https://app.eu.cleartax.com/new-einvoicing/
- Click **Create an account**, enter your work email, and set a strong password.
- Accept the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions, then click **Agree and continue**.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/login-screen.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Login screen" />

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/create-an-account.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Create an account" />

**Step 2: Verify your email**

- A verification link is sent to your email. Open it to verify your account.
- If you can't find it, check your Spam folder or click **Resend Email**.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/verify-your-email.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Verify your email" />

- After verification, log in and accept the policies once more if prompted.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/accept-policies.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Accept policies" />

**Step 3: Create your workspace**

- Enter your **Company legal name**.
- Select your company's turnover range (&lt;25k EUR, 25k - 100k EUR, &gt;100k EUR).
- Click **Continue**.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/create-your-workspace.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Create your workspace" />

**Step 4: Personalise your experience**

- Confirm your email, enter your name, and pick a designation (CFO/Director, Head/VP/GM, Manager, Other).
- Click **Continue**. You'll see "Workspace created successfully".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/help-us-personalise-your-experience.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Help us personalise your experience" />

## Register your business

**Step 1: Open e-Invoicing**

- On the Clear Compliance Cloud home page, click the **e-Invoicing** tile.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/e-invoicing-tile.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="e-Invoicing tile" />

- On **Say hello to E-invoicing**, click **Continue**.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/welcome-step.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Welcome step" />

**Step 2: Select Norway**

- Choose **Norway** as your first country and click **Add country**.
- You can add more countries later from **Settings &gt; Business settings**.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/select-your-first-country.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Select your first country" />

**Step 3: Add business details**

- **Organisation Identifier (VAT or Group VAT):** your Norwegian VAT number.
- **Organisation Name:** your registered legal name.
- Click **Continue to Dashboard**.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**Norwegian VAT number format:** NO + 9-digit organisation number + MVA, for example NO999999905MVA. Any other format is rejected
</Callout>

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/invalid-vat-number-error.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Invalid VAT number error" />

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/valid-vat-number.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Valid VAT number" />

Once done, you land on the e-Invoicing home page. It stays empty until you import documents.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/onboarding/empty-home-page-after-onboarding.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Empty home page after onboarding" />

## Peppol ID registration (manual)

To receive e-invoices over Peppol, your business needs a Peppol Participant ID registered on the Peppol network.

- Share your organisation's legal name and organisation number with the ClearTax team so they can enable registration.
- Your Peppol ID is based on your Norwegian organisation number, using scheme 0192. Example: 0192:999999905.
- ClearTax, as your Access Point, registers the ID on Peppol which makes you discoverable to other Peppol participants.
- Verify your registration at the Peppol Directory (https://directory.peppol.eu/public) by searching your organisation number or legal name.
