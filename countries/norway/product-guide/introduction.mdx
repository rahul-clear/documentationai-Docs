## The Norwegian B2B e-invoicing mandate

Norway already requires e-invoices for sales to the public sector (B2G) through EHF on Peppol. The amended Bookkeeping Act extends this to business-to-business trade.

| Milestone                           | Date                | What it means                                                                                                                   |
|-------------------------------------|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| B2G e-invoicing                     | In force since 2019 | Suppliers to public bodies send EHF invoices over Peppol                                                                        |
| B2B sending obligation              | 1 January 2027      | Bookkeeping-liable businesses must send structured e-invoices to bookkeeping-liable recipients registered on the Peppol network |
| B2B receiving + digital bookkeeping | 1 January 2030      | Businesses must be able to receive e-invoices, and accounting systems must support automated processing                         |

A **structured e-invoice** is a machine-readable XML document that can be processed automatically. A PDF sent by email is **not** a compliant e-invoice under the new rules.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**Out of scope:** Consumer (B2C) and cash sales are not covered by the B2B obligation.
</Callout>

## The Peppol 4-corner model

Norway does not use a government clearance portal. Invoices move directly between trading partners over the Peppol network, through certified Access Points.

| Corner     | Party                            | Role                                               |
|------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| C1         | Seller                           | Creates the invoice in its ERP or in ClearTax      |
| C2         | Seller's Access Point (ClearTax) | Validates the invoice and transmits it over Peppol |
| C3         | Buyer's Access Point             | Receives the invoice and passes it on              |
| C4         | Buyer                            | Receives the invoice in its ERP or AP system       |

Think of it like mobile networks: you can call anyone regardless of their operator, because operators connect to each other. Peppol Access Points work the same way.

## ClearTax as your Access Point

ClearTax acts as your Peppol Access Point (corner 2 for sales, corner 3 for purchases). It:

- Validates every invoice against EHF / Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 rules before sending
- Looks up the receiver's Peppol ID in the Peppol SMP
- Transmits documents securely over Peppol (AS4)
- Receives supplier invoices sent to your Peppol ID and shows them in your Purchase Documents inbox
- Tracks delivery status end to end, from submission to delivery
