Norway is moving B2B invoicing onto structured e-invoices. Under the amended Bookkeeping Act, bookkeeping-liable businesses must **send** structured e-invoices from **1 January 2027**, with receiving obligations and digital bookkeeping following from **1 January 2030**. The exchange runs on the **Peppol** network, and ClearTax connects you to it as your Peppol Access Point.

This guide walks you through the ClearTax e-Invoicing product for Norway, from signing up to sending, receiving and reporting on invoices.

## Key details

| Detail                | Norway                                                                                   |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Legal basis         | Bookkeeping Act (bokføringsloven) §10, as amended by LOV-2026-06-19-39                   |
| Policy owner        | Ministry of Finance, with the Norwegian Agency for Public and Financial Management (DFØ) |
| Network             | Peppol (4-corner model)                                                                  |
| Recipient register  | Peppol SMP, with participants listed in the Peppol Directory                             |
| Format              | EHF / Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (EN 16931)                                                  |
| Business identifier | 9-digit organisation number (Peppol scheme 0192)                                         |
| VAT number format   | NO + 9-digit organisation number + MVA, e.g. NO999999905MVA                              |
| Currency in product | NOK (amounts shown in kr)                                                                |
| Applies to          | B2B invoices between bookkeeping-liable businesses (B2G has been on EHF since 2019)      |
