You work with two dashboard-level screens every day:

1. **Clear Finance Cloud landing page:** the launcher for every Clear product. You pass through it to enter e-Invoicing.
2. **E-Invoicing home:** the working home page inside the e-Invoicing app.

## Clear Finance Cloud landing page

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/dashboard-walkthrough/clear-finance-cloud-landing-page.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="Clear Finance Cloud landing page" />

### Header bar

| Element                         | Purpose                                                  |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| App switcher (top-left)         | Return to this landing page from anywhere in the product |
| Language toggle                 | Switch the UI language                                   |
| Ask Support                     | Contact ClearTax support                                 |
| Bell icon                       | System notifications                                     |
| Workspace name pill (top-right) | Opens your account and workspace menu                    |

### Workspace and account menu

Click the workspace pill in the top-right to open your account panel:

- Your avatar, name and account email
- **Account & Security Settings:** password, MFA and session management
- **Your workspaces:** every workspace you belong to. Click **Manage** to open workspace settings
- **Logout of all workspaces**

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/dashboard-walkthrough/workspace-and-account-menu.png" width="2048" height="1149" alt="Workspace and account menu" />

### Product cards and right rail

- **e-Invoicing** ("Generate E-invoices within minutes"): click to enter the e-Invoicing home.
- **Star + workspace name** confirms the workspace you're in, and **Go to settings** jumps straight to workspace settings.

## E-Invoicing home

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/norway/product-guide/dashboard-walkthrough/e-invoicing-home-norway.jpg" width="1400" height="803" alt="E-Invoicing home (Norway)" />

### Top bar

| Element               | Purpose                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| App switcher          | Return to the Clear Finance Cloud landing page                                                                           |
| Country selector (NO) | Visible for multi-country customers. Switch between Norway and your other markets (e.g. Belgium, Poland, UAE)            |
| VAT / branch selector | Shows the current business and its VAT (NO…MVA). Click to switch business or branch; all data rescopes to your selection |
| Language toggle (EN)  | Same as the landing page                                                                                                 |
| Support Desk          | Raise a support ticket without leaving the product (see [Support Desk](/countries/norway/product-guide/support-desk))                                                    |
| Bell icon             | E-Invoicing notifications                                                                                                |
| Workspace pill        | Same account menu as the landing page                                                                                    |

### Left sidebar

| Label              | What it does                                                                     |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Home               | Returns to the e-Invoicing home                                                  |
| Sales Documents    | Manage all outbound e-invoices (see [B2B Sales Documents](/countries/norway/product-guide/sales-documents))                         |
| Purchase Documents | Invoices received over Peppol and self-billed documents (see [Purchase Documents](/countries/norway/product-guide/purchase-documents)) |
| Reports            | MIS Dashboard and analytics (see [MIS Dashboard](/countries/norway/product-guide/mis-dashboard))                                  |
| Import             | Bulk upload sales or purchase documents                                          |
| Import history     | Audit trail of past imports                                                      |
| Settings (bottom)  | Workspace, users, templates and integrations (see [Settings](/countries/norway/product-guide/settings))                      |

### Home page tiles

| Tile                      | What it does                                                                                                                                   |
|---------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Sales documents           | Last 30 days summary: total ingested, Pending, Failed, Submitted to exchange network, Delivered to buyer network, Delivered to buyer, Received |
| Purchase documents        | Last 30 days summary of received and self-billed documents                                                                                     |
| MIS Dashboard             | Detailed insights and breakdown of sales and purchase documents                                                                                |
| Product guides and videos | Opens the guides library                                                                                                                       |
| Announcements             | Product and regulatory announcements, e.g. Peppol updates                                                                                      |
