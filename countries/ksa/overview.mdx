---

Saudi Arabia's ZATCA e-invoicing mandate requires all VAT-registered businesses to generate, sign, and (for B2B) clear or (for B2C) report electronic invoices in a standardised format.

<Image src="/images/ksa/hero.png" alt="ZATCA e-invoicing clearance model diagram" />

*This page covers how sales invoices flow through ClearTax under the clearance/reporting model, and API reference material further down.*

**Regulatory basis:** Saudi Arabia's e-invoicing mandate (FATOORAH) is run by ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority) in two phases: Phase 1 (Generation), effective 4 Dec 2021, requiring electronic generation and storage of invoices; and Phase 2 (Integration), rolled out in waves since 1 Jan 2023, requiring integration with ZATCA's platform, with each wave notified at least six months ahead of its integration date. Source: [ZATCA - E-Invoicing](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Pages/default.aspx)
