## Navigating the Purchase Dashboard

The "Purchase Documents" section is your automated inbox for Peppol.

This feature is a direct benefit of your Peppol registration. When you complete your onboarding and share your Peppol ID with your suppliers, any e-invoice they send to that ID from their Peppol Access Point appears in your Purchase Dashboard.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/belgium/product-guide/purchase-documents/purchase-documents-1.png" width="1600" height="858" alt="Purchase documents" />

- Detailed View: Clicking on the invoice number, the details of the invoice are shown.
- Validations: All received invoices are automatically validated against Peppol standards.

## Actions on Purchase Document

The following are the actions that can be perfomed on purchase documents in your inbox.

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Download XML        | Downloads a ZIP file containing the XML files exchanged over the Peppol network.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Download CSV Report | Downloads selected lite/detailed report containing the details of the invoice<br />• Light Report: Contains fewer fields, suitable for image consumption.<br />• Detailed Report: Contains significantly more fields (approx. 74 fields). |
| Print               | Generates a PDF of the invoice using the available template.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Email               | Send the invoice to the buyer's email address using a selected template.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Show/Hide Column    | Shows/Hides the column basis preference                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Sort                | Sorts the column basis preference                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
