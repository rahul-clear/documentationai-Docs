## Sales Side Flow

<Expandable title="B2B" default-open="true">
  ClearTax validates each invoice against European (EN 16931) and Belgian national validation rules, then delivers it directly to your buyer's certified Peppol Access Point. Outbound invoices are issued in standard Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (UBL 2.1) format, covering all required document types under the Belgian mandate (invoices, credit notes, debit notes).

## Purchase Side Flow

Invoices arriving from your suppliers' Access Points over the Peppol network are accepted in mandate-compliant EN 16931 formats (primarily Peppol BIS 3.0 UBL), normalized into a common internal representation, and made available on the platform for your AP processing.
