## Prerequisites

Before you start exchanging e-invoices for Croatia, make sure you have:

1. **OIB:** Your valid 11-digit Personal Identification Number. This is the primary key for the Fiscalisation System and the AMS address book.
2. **Fina/AKD application digital certificate:** A digital certificate from an authorised certificate authority (Fina or AKD) containing your OIB. You need it to access the ePorezna portal.
3. **Authorisation in the ePorezna portal:** ClearTax's certified Information Intermediary must be authorised to submit fiscalisation and e-reporting data on your behalf in the ePorezna (Tax Administration) portal. Reach out to your ClearTax account representative with the required details and they will guide you through this step. Without this authorisation, requests are rejected with error S006.
4. **AMS registration:** Your business must be registered in the AMS (Address Book of Metadata Services) so that you can receive e-invoices. This registration is initiated on your behalf using your OIB and business name.

## Onboarding steps

| Step | Owner                                          | Action                                                                                                                                             |
|------|------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | You                                            | Obtain your Fina/AKD digital certificate and confirm your OIB.                                                                                     |
| 2    | You, with your ClearTax account representative | Reach out to your ClearTax account representative with the required details. They will guide you through the authorisation in the ePorezna portal. |
| 3    | ClearTax                                       | Initiate AMS registration using your OIB and business name, so that e-invoices can be routed to you.                                               |
| 4    | ClearTax                                       | Set up your workspace and ERP integration.                                                                                                         |
| 5    | ClearTax and you                               | Testing phase - we recommend at least 2 days of stable operation in the test environment.                                                          |
| 6    | ClearTax                                       | Switch your workspace to production.                                                                                                               |

## Create your profile

First, sign up on the ClearTax portal:

- Enter a valid email address (preferably the admin email ID) and choose a strong password. You will receive a verification link at this address.
- Select the Terms and Conditions checkbox and click "Agree and Continue".
- Open the verification email and click "Verify email address". This opens ClearTax in a new tab and starts workspace creation.
- Enter your company's legal name and choose a turnover range to create your workspace, then click "Continue".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/croatia/product-guide/onboarding/create-workspace.png" width="3024" height="1654" alt="Create your workspace" />

- Provide your name and designation to complete your profile, then click "Continue". You can also skip this step and complete it later from Settings.
- On the Clear Compliance Cloud home page, click the e-Invoicing tile to start your Croatia e-invoicing journey.

## Register your business

### Step 1 - Welcome

Review the product highlights and click "Continue".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/croatia/product-guide/onboarding/welcome.png" width="3024" height="1654" alt="Onboarding Step 1 - Welcome" />

### Step 2 - Add country

Select Croatia as your first country and click "Add country". You can add more countries later from the Business settings page.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/croatia/product-guide/onboarding/select-country.png" width="3024" height="1654" alt="Onboarding Step 2 - Select Croatia as your country" />

### Step 3 - Create business profile

Enter your Organisation Identifier and Organisation Name, then click "Continue to Dashboard".

- **Organisation Identifier (VAT or Group VAT):** your Croatian VAT number - HR followed by your 11-digit OIB (for example, HR99991123245).
- **Organisation Name:** your registered business name.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/croatia/product-guide/onboarding/business-profile.png" width="3024" height="1654" alt="Onboarding Step 3 - Create business profile" />

Your business is organised as OIB/VAT, with branches below it. Users must be mapped to an OIB to perform fiscal actions.

## Adding more businesses

You can add more VATs, branches, or countries to your workspace from Settings → Business settings.

- Use the "Add / Switch Country" dropdown to choose Croatia. Your businesses are listed with their VAT and branch codes.
- Click "Add business" to add a new VAT, or "Add Branch" against an existing VAT to add a branch.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/croatia/product-guide/onboarding/business-settings.png" width="3024" height="1654" alt="Business settings - Croatia" />

- In the "Add new VATs" panel, enter the VAT and Organisation Name for each business, then click "Save".

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/croatia/product-guide/onboarding/add-new-vats.png" width="3024" height="1654" alt="Add new VATs" />
