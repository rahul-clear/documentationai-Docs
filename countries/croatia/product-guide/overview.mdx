Step by step guides on how to use the Peppol based ClearTax E-Invoicing solution for Croatia, covering e-invoice exchange, fiscalisation, and the monthly e-reporting obligations introduced by Fiscalisation 2.0.

| Detail              | Information                                                                              |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Tax authority       | Tax Administration (Porezna uprava), Ministry of Finance                                 |
| Government platform | eFiskalizacija (Fiscalisation System) and eReporting                                     |
| Mandate             | Fiscalisation 2.0 - e-invoice exchange, real-time fiscalisation, and monthly e-reporting |
| Network             | Peppol, via a certified Information Intermediary                                         |
| Format              | UBL 2.1 (HR-CIUS) with Croatian extensions                                               |
| Tax identifier      | OIB (11-digit Personal Identification Number)                                            |
| Currency            | EUR                                                                                      |
| Applies to          | Domestic B2B invoices (B2G already live)                                                 |
