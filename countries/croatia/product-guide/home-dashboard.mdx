---
Once onboarding is complete, you land on the e-Invoicing home page. The top bar shows the country selector (HR) and the business and VAT number you are working in - click it to switch businesses.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/croatia/product-guide/home-dashboard/home-page.png" width="3024" height="1648" alt="e-Invoicing home page" />
- **Sales documents** and **Purchase documents** - summary cards for your outgoing and incoming documents. Click the arrow to open the full list.
- **Business insights and analytics** - the MIS Dashboard, with detailed insights and a breakdown of sales and purchase documents.
- **Reconciliation** - SR vs Einvoice recon (Sales Register vs Sales e-invoice) and PR vs Einvoice recon (Purchase Register vs Purchase e-invoice).
- **Help and support** - Product guides and videos.
- **Announcements** - product announcements from the ClearTax team.

Tiles marked with a padlock are paid add-ons. To enable them, contact your ClearTax point of contact.
