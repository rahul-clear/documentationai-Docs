---
title: Purchase Documents
description: "Why purchase documents aren't applicable for Jordan"
---
JoFotara only exposes Sales Document / Send APIs to ClearTax - no purchase document or Fetch APIs have been made available by the government.

As a result, the **Purchase Documents** feature does not apply for Jordan. If you operate in other countries as well, Purchase Documents will continue to work as expected for those jurisdictions - this limitation is specific to the Jordan (JoFotara) integration.
