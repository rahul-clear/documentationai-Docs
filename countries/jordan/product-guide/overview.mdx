This product guide walks through the ClearTax Jordan E-Invoicing product end to end - from creating your workspace and connecting it to JoFotara, through day-to-day use of the dashboard, Sales Documents, bulk imports, reporting, workspace settings, and the built-in support desk.

Jordan's Income and Sales Tax Department (ISTD) requires VAT-registered businesses to clear every invoice in real time through **JoFotara**, the government's e-invoicing platform. ClearTax connects directly to JoFotara so your invoices are generated and cleared automatically - no manual portal work needed.

| Detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Tax authority | ISTD (Income & Sales Tax Department) |
| Invoice portal | JoFotara |
| Clearance model | Real-time clearance |
| Applies to | B2B, B2C, and B2G invoices |
