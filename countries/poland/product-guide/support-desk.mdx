Raise and track tickets with ClearTax support without leaving the product: a stuck submission, a KSeF error, a certificate issue or a feature request.

- **Raise ticket** - describe your issue, attach a screenshot, and submit
- **All tickets** - view the status of everything you've raised

## Getting to Support Desk

Click Support Desk in the top bar (between the language toggle and the notifications bell).

## The Support Desk panel

| Card         | Purpose                              | Shortcut     |
|--------------|--------------------------------------|--------------|
| Raise ticket | Opens the ticket form                | Ctrl + Q     |
| All tickets  | Lists your active and closed tickets | \-           |

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/poland/product-guide/support-desk/support-desk-panel.png" width="2048" height="1149" alt="Support Desk panel" />

## Raising a ticket

- **Screenshot capture** - auto-captured when the form opens. Use Capture screenshot or Add more attachments (invoice XML, error reports) as needed.
- **Issue subject** (required) - one-line summary
- **Issue description** (required) - what you did, what you expected, what happened, and any error messages
- **Call-back contact number** (optional) - country code default
- **Additional contacts in CC** (optional) - comma-separated emails

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/poland/product-guide/support-desk/raise-a-ticket-form.png" width="2048" height="1149" alt="Raise a ticket form" />

Click **Raise ticket** to submit. Updates come by email. ClearTax categorises and routes the ticket internally, so you don't need to pick a category or priority.

## Tracking tickets

All tickets shows tickets raised in the last 90 days. For older requests, contact the ClearTax support team directly.
