Reports give you an aggregated view of your e-invoicing activity: volumes, status distribution, day-wise trends and financial totals, across both Sales and Purchase documents.

## Getting to Reports

Click the Reports icon in the left sidebar to land on the MIS Dashboard.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/poland/product-guide/reports/reports-mis-dashboard-card.jpg" width="1280" height="731" alt="Reports > MIS Dashboard card" />

## The MIS Dashboard

Five zones: tab (Sales), filter controls, KPI cards, charts, and the Overview table. KPI cards show Invoices ingested, Valid invoices, Total valid invoices value (PLN) and Total valid invoices tax amount (PLN).

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/poland/product-guide/reports/mis-dashboard-sales-tab.png" width="2048" height="1149" alt="MIS Dashboard, Sales tab" />

### Filter panel

| Filter group        | Options                                                           |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Document date range | Start and end date (DD/MM/YY)                                     |
| Document status     | Pending, Submitted, Valid, Failed                                 |
| Mode of generation  | Manual ingestion - Web, API, SFTP                                 |
| Document type       | Tax invoice, Debit note, Credit note, Pre-payment invoice, Others |

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/poland/product-guide/reports/mis-dashboard-filter-panel.png" width="2048" height="1149" alt="MIS Dashboard filter panel" />

### Status breakdown chart

A half-donut chart of documents by status in the selected window. Hover over a segment to see its count.

### Daywise breakdown chart

A stacked bar chart of daily volume by status. A spike in Failed on a given day usually points to an ERP, integration or KSeF availability issue.

### Overview table

Each row is one entity (Entity Name + NIP). Columns are grouped by status (Total, Pending, Submitted, Valid, Failed), each with:

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/poland/product-guide/reports/overview-table-illustrative-values.png" width="2048" height="206" alt="Overview table (illustrative values)" />

| Sub-column                | Meaning                           |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Invoice                   | Count of documents in this status |
| Total invoice value (PLN) | Sum of gross amounts              |
| Total tax amount (PLN)    | Sum of VAT                        |
