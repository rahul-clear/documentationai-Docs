Malaysia's e-invoicing mandate is administered by LHDN (Inland Revenue Board) via the MyInvois platform. ClearTax submits structured invoices to LHDN on your behalf, with optional sharing over the Peppol network.

This page covers how sales and purchase invoices flow through ClearTax to LHDN (see [Flows](/countries/malaysia/flows)), and API and platform reference material (see [Setup & Reference](/countries/malaysia/setup-reference)).

**Regulatory basis:** e-Invoicing is administered by LHDN (Lembaga Hasil Dalam Negeri) in phases by annual turnover: businesses above RM100 million from 1 August 2024, RM25-100 million from 1 January 2025, RM5-25 million from 1 July 2025, and RM1-5 million from 1 January 2026. Source: [LHDN - e-Invoice](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/e-invoice/)
