## Create your profile

You can create your profile on ClearTax website for e-invoicing generation & management through the below links -

Sandbox - [https://app-sandbox.my.cleartax.com/](https://app-sandbox.my.cleartax.com/)

Production - [https://app.my.cleartax.com/](https://app.my.cleartax.com/)

Once you land on the ClearTax Malaysia e-invoicing webpage, you need to create your profile.

First, sign-up or register on the ClearTax portal using the following steps:

- Enter a valid email address and choose a strong password on the page for successful registration.
- You can use any valid email address where you will receive the verification link for your account.
- Select the checkbox beside 'Terms and Conditions" privacy checkbox.
- Click on "Agree and Continue" to sign-up and create your account on ClearTax.

You will receive a verification email on the email address you used above for signing up on ClearTax.

- Go to your mailbox, open the email and click on 'Verify email address' to complete the verification.
- This will open ClearTax in a new tab and start with the process of workspace creation.

Now, you need to create your workspace

- Enter the details of your company like legal name and choose a turnover range to create your workspace.
- Workspace will be where you can access ClearTax and all your data.
- You can invite multiple colleagues to your workspace and also generate E-Invoices, archive data, reconcile and use different features of ClearTax Compliance cloud.
- After entering your details please click on 'Continue' to proceed to the next step.

Once workspace creation is completed, it is time for profile creation with more information about you so that we can create a role for you.

- This will help in creating a personalized profile that will help us in optimizing your work.
- Please provide your name and designation in your organization.
- After entering your details please click on 'Continue' to proceed to the next step.
- You can also skip this step and enter it later from the 'Settings' page.

Once the details are entered and your workspace is created, you will land on the home page of 'Clear Compliance Cloud'. It has multiple features to help you with E-Invoicing and compliance. Click on 'Continue' to explore the features.

Click on e-Invoicing tile to start your Malaysia e-invoicing journey and generate LHDN compliant e-invoices.

## Register your business

Once you have created your profile on ClearTax, you need to register your business to start with Malaysia e-invoicing.

- Once you click on the e-Invoicing tile on the homepage, you will land on the Welcome page to get started.
- Click on the 'Continue' button to proceed with your business profile creation.

Create your business profile by adding your business details

1. TIN (Tax Identification Number)
   1. Enter a valid business TIN assigned by IRBM
   2. Mandatory field
2. Business registration number
   1. Mandatory field
3. SST registration number
   1. Mandatory for SST registrant
   2. This is not applicable to businesses that are not SST-registered.
4. Tourism Tax Registration Number
   1. Mandatory for Tourism tax registrant
   2. This is only applicable to tourism tax registrants, which may consist of hotel operators and online travel operators.

**Note: Please remove any leading 0s after the first character in the TIN**

- Once you have entered your business details, click on the 'Finish' button to move forward. You have successfully completed your onboarding process.
- You will land on the homepage for Malaysia e-invoicing where you can start your e-invoicing journey.

For successful e-invoice generation via ClearTax, you need to register ClearTax as an intermediary on MyInvois portal. Refer to the guide "[Add ClearTax as an intermediary on MyInvois portal](/countries/malaysia/product-guide/myinvois-intermediary)" for steps on the same.\
