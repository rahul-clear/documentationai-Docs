Step by step guides on how to use the ClearTax E-Invoicing solution for Malaysia to generate LHDN compliant e-invoices.

| Detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Tax authority | LHDN / IRBM (Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia) |
| Government portal | MyInvois |
| ClearTax role | Intermediary on your MyInvois profile |
| Document types | Sales e-invoices, consolidated e-invoices, and self-billed e-invoices |
