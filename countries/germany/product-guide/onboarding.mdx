## Create your profile

You can create your profile on ClearTax website for e-invoicing generation & management through the below link -

Production - [https://app.eu.cleartax.com/new-einvoicing/](https://app.eu.cleartax.com/new-einvoicing/)

Once you land on the ClearTax EU e-invoicing webpage, you need to create your profile.

First, sign-up or register on the ClearTax portal using the following steps:

- Enter a valid email address and choose a strong password on the page for successful registration.

- You can use a valid email address (preferably the admin email id) where you will receive the verification link for your account.

- Select the checkbox beside 'Terms and Conditions" privacy checkbox.

- Click on "Agree and Continue" to sign-up and create your account on ClearTax.

You will receive a verification email on the email address you used above for signing up on ClearTax.

- Go to your mailbox, open the email and click on 'Verify email address' to complete the verification.

- This will open ClearTax in a new tab and start with the process of workspace creation.

Now, you need to create your workspace

- Enter the details of your company like legal name and choose a turnover range to create your workspace.

- Workspace will be where you can access ClearTax and all your data.

- You can invite multiple colleagues to your workspace and also generate E-Invoices and use different features of ClearTax Compliance cloud.

- After entering your details please click on 'Continue' to proceed to the next step.

Once workspace creation is completed, it is time for profile creation with more information about you so that we can create a role for you.

- This will help in creating a personalized profile that will help us in optimizing your work.

- Please provide your name and designation in your organization.

- You can also skip this step and enter it later from the 'Settings' page.

Once the details are entered and your workspace is created, you will land on the home page of 'Clear Compliance Cloud'. Click on 'Continue' to explore the features.

Click on e-Invoicing tile to start your Germany e-invoicing journey and generate compliant e-invoices.

## Register your business

Once you have created your profile on ClearTax, you need to select your primary country and register your business to start with Germany e-invoicing.

- Once you click on the e-Invoicing tile on the homepage, you will land on the Welcome page to get started.

- Click on the 'Continue' button to proceed with your country selection and business profile creation.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/germany/product-guide/onboarding/register-your-business.jpg" width="1400" height="742" alt="Say hello to e-Invoicing" />

Amongst the list of countries, choose your primary country as 'Germany'.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/germany/product-guide/onboarding/register-your-business-2.jpg" width="1400" height="796" alt="Select Germany as your first country" />

After selecting the country, create your business profile by adding your business details.

1. Organization Name: Your registered business name

2. VAT Number: Your official German VAT identification number (e.g., DE123456789). VAT numbers are validated against country-specific formats (Germany requires DE followed by nine digits). An incorrect format will trigger an error.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/germany/product-guide/onboarding/register-your-business-3.jpg" width="1400" height="797" alt="Add business details" />

Once you have entered your business details, click on the 'Finish' button to move forward. You have successfully completed your onboarding process.

You will land on the homepage for Germany e-invoicing where you can start your e-invoicing journey.

## Peppol ID Registration (Manual)

To register on Peppol network, the ClearTax team will enable your registration using the information below:

1. Organization legal name

2. VAT number, or Leitweg-ID for public sector entities

*Table 2: Examples citing German Peppol Participant ID Schemes*

| **Identifier**                   | **Scheme ID** | **Example**  |
|----------------------------------|---------------|--------------|
| German VAT Number                | 9930          | DE123456789  |
| Leitweg-ID (Public Sector / B2G) | 0204          | 991-12345-67 |

Basis the provided information, a Peppol Participant ID is created for your business. This ID is your unique, public address on the network- it functions like a phone number for e-invoicing. In Germany, this ID is typically based on your VAT number (scheme 9930), or on the Leitweg-ID (scheme 0204) for public administration entities.

ClearTax, as your Access Point, registers this new ID in the Peppol Directory (SMP - Service Metadata Publisher) on your behalf. This registration makes your business "discoverable" to all other participants on the network, enabling them to send e-invoices to you.

### View Your Peppol Configuration

After the registration is complete, you can view your active Peppol configuration on the public peppol directory - [https://directory.peppol.eu/public](https://directory.peppol.eu/public).

Here, you can search by your peppol id or company legal name and see your details.

This ID is the single most important piece of information for receiving invoices. You must now share this Peppol ID with all your suppliers and request that they send all future e-invoices to this address.
