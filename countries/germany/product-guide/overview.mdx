Germany is phasing in mandatory structured e-invoicing for all domestic B2B transactions. The law strictly regulates invoice **formats** (requiring EN 16931 standards like XRechnung, ZUGFeRD, and Peppol BIS), but leaves the delivery method up to you.

**Key Deadlines**

- **January 1, 2025:** All businesses must be able to **receive** structured e-invoices.

- **January 1, 2027:** Businesses with prior-year turnover above €800,000 must start **issuing** them.

- **January 1, 2028:** All businesses must **issue** them.

**How ClearTax Helps** ClearTax supports all mandatory German formats and sends e-invoices directly via email or the Peppol network (as a certified Peppol Access Point).

**Key Information:**

| **Detail**       | **Information**                                                                     |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Tax authority    | Federal Ministry of Finance (Bundesministerium der Finanzen, BMF)                   |
| Exchange channel | Not mandated – Peppol, email or any other channel agreed with the trading partner   |
| Exchange model   | Decentralised, no government clearance (Peppol 4-corner model when sent via Peppol) |
| Format           | XRechnung, ZUGFeRD hybrid PDF, Peppol BIS 3.0 (UBL, EN 16931)                       |
| Applies to       | Domestic B2B invoices between businesses established in Germany                     |
