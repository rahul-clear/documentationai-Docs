## Sales Side Flow

<Expandable title="B2B (flow diagram)" default-open="true">
  Domestic B2B invoices are validated by ClearTax, filed with DGFiP through the PPF, and delivered to the buyer's platform in UBL format. All document types recognised under the French mandate are supported, and every status change the mandate requires reporting on is filed with the PPF within a 24-hour window.

<Expandable title="B2C" default-open="true">
  Transactions outside domestic B2B scope - cross-border sales and purchases, B2C transactions, and related payment reporting - are handled through e-reporting. Each transaction is submitted to ClearTax on a per-invoice basis; ClearTax consolidates and submits it to the PPF within DGFiP's mandated reporting timelines.

## Purchase Side Flow

Invoices arriving from suppliers' platforms are accepted in any mandate-approved format (UBL, CII, or Factur-X), normalised into a common representation, and made available on the platform. Businesses can manage the full purchase-invoice lifecycle as defined by the mandate, with all required actions reported to DGFiP.
