---
title: Switching Your PA to ClearTax
description: 'How to move your Approved Platform (PA) to ClearTax with no gap in sending or receiving e-invoices'
---
Changing your Approved Platform means telling the central Annuaire (the State directory that routes e-invoices) that ClearTax, and not your previous PA, now receives invoices at your electronic addresses. It is a coordinated, low-effort change: you sign a designation mandate and connect to us, and ClearTax handles the directory update and a clean cutover so you keep sending and receiving throughout.

## The switch at a glance

You designate ClearTax as your new PA and name your former PA → ClearTax updates your receiving addresses in the Annuaire from an agreed effective date → routing moves to ClearTax with no gap.

## Who does what

### What you do

1. **Tell us you want to switch.** Sign the onboarding agreement with ClearTax and confirm the go-live (effective) date you want.
2. **Share your details.** Your SIREN, the establishments (SIRET) and any routing codes, and the electronic addresses you wish to use for sending/receiving invoices that are currently handled by your existing PA.
3. **Sign the PA designation mandate.** A short formal document naming ClearTax as your new Approved Platform, listing your former PA, the effective date, and the scope of receiving addresses. We will provide the template - you sign it.
4. **Complete technical onboarding.** Connect to ClearTax and validate a test exchange so you can send and receive.
5. **Honour any notice with your old PA.** Give notice per your existing contract; we align the cutover date so there is no gap.

### What ClearTax does

1. **Onboard you.** Set up your account, capture the full scope of receiving addresses and confirm the effective date.
2. **Update the Annuaire (directory).** Register your electronic receiving addresses under ClearTax - across all your address forms (SIREN, SIREN_SIRET, routing code, suffix) - effective from the agreed date, superseding your former PA's entries.
3. **Confirm routing is live.** Verify the directory lines are active and that inbound invoices now route to ClearTax, with no overlap or gap versus the old PA.
4. **Protect continuity.** Coordinate the cutover so in-flight documents are not lost or duplicated during the changeover.
5. **Confirm go-live.** Notify you once the switch is complete and you are fully operational on ClearTax.

## Timing & continuity

Plan for a short lead time (typically a couple of weeks) to cover onboarding, the signed mandate and the directory update.

The old and new directory entries can be aligned on the effective date so there is no window where invoices are unrouted.

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
A directory entry **cannot take effect the same day** - a new date must be at least D+1.
</Callout>
