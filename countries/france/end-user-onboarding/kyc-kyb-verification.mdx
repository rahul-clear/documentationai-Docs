As part of our regulatory obligations, we verify the identity of individual customers (KYC - Know Your Customer) and the registration details of business customers (KYB - Know Your Business) before onboarding. This guide explains what to expect, what you'll need to have ready, and the steps involved.

## 1. Overview

There are two verification tracks, depending on whether you are being verified as an individual or on behalf of a registered company:

- **Business Verification (KYB):** for companies registered in France. We confirm your company's registration against official French business registries (RCS / INSEE) and screen the company and its officers for compliance risk.
- **Individual Verification (KYC):** for individual customers, or for the person(s) who legally represent a business (e.g. company directors) fetched from your KYB. This confirms your identity using a government-issued ID and a short facial liveness check.

Both checks are carried out using a secure, encrypted verification link. You do not need to create an account or install any software - everything is completed through your web browser.

## 2. What You'll Need

Before you start, please have the following ready:

| For Business Verification (KYB) | For Individual Verification (KYC) |
| --- | --- |
| A copy of your Extrait K-Bis | A valid government-issued photo ID (see accepted documents below) |
| A smartphone, tablet or computer with a working camera, or you can upload the Extrait K-Bis as well | A smartphone, tablet or computer with a working camera |
| Access to the email address you'll use to receive the verification link | A well-lit space, and a few minutes free from interruption |

### Accepted documents

| Check type | Accepted documents |
| --- | --- |
| Individual (KYC) | French national ID card / eID card, biometric passport, or residence permit |
| Business (KYB) | Official RCS/INSEE registry data: **Extrait K-Bis record** |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**Tip:** Please make sure the document type you plan to use is supported before you start - using an unsupported or mismatched document is the most common reason a verification doesn't go through on the first attempt.
</Callout>

## 3. Business Verification (KYB) - Step by Step

### Step 1 - You'll receive a secure link

We will send a unique verification link to your registered email address. This link is personal to your case and should not be shared.

### Step 2 - Review and accept the consent notice

When you open the link, you'll be asked to confirm you're 16 or older and to consent to Shufti Pro processing your data for verification purposes, in line with their Privacy Policy.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/end-user-onboarding/kyb-instructions.jpg" width="2010" height="1048" alt="KYB document upload instructions" />

### Step 3 - Select and upload your company document

Choose the document type you're providing (Extrait K-Bis). You can either upload a clear photo/PDF of the document or take a picture of it directly using your camera - both front and back if applicable.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/end-user-onboarding/kyb-upload-kbis.jpg" width="2010" height="1048" alt="Upload the photo of your Extrait K-Bis" />

### Step 4 - Submit and wait for confirmation

Once submitted, you'll see a "Verification Results Awaiting Approval" screen. You can safely close this page - there is nothing further for you to do at this stage.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/end-user-onboarding/verification-awaiting-approval.jpg" width="2010" height="1048" alt="Verification Results Awaiting Approval screen" />

### Step 5 - We complete the review

Our team cross-checks your company's registration details against the official French business registry and reviews the company officers on file. **If your company officers list doesn't show an active legal representative, we may reach out and ask you to provide an updated K-Bis identifying the Président, Directeur Général, or Gérant, as applicable, so that person can also complete an individual verification (see Section 4).**

## 4. Individual Verification (KYC) - Step by Step

### Step 1 - You'll receive a secure link

We will send a personalised verification link to your email address. This link is valid for a limited time, so please complete it soon after receiving it.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/end-user-onboarding/kyc-consent.jpg" width="2010" height="1048" alt="Let's verify your identity - consent checkbox" />

### Step 2 - Review the instructions and grant camera access

You'll see a short screen with guidance (good lighting, no sunglasses or hats) and a consent checkbox. Once you agree, your browser will ask permission to access your camera - please allow this to continue.

### Step 3 - Choose your document

Select France as the issuing country and choose your document type: National ID card, Passport, or Driver's Licence.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/end-user-onboarding/kyc-choose-document.jpg" width="1330" height="996" alt="Choose your document issuing country and document type" />

### Step 4 - Capture your document

Follow the on-screen prompts to photograph or upload your document, fitting it within the frame shown. Avoid glare, blur, or an expired document - you can retry if the first attempt doesn't come out clearly.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/end-user-onboarding/kyc-capture-document.jpg" width="1330" height="996" alt="Document capture instructions" />

### Step 5 - Complete the facial liveness check

You'll be asked to position your face within the frame so the system can confirm you're a real, live person (not a photo or video) and match your face to your ID document.

### Step 6 - Submit and wait for confirmation

Once complete, you'll see a "Verification Results Awaiting Approval" screen confirming your submission was received. You can close the page at this point.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/end-user-onboarding/verification-awaiting-approval.jpg" width="2010" height="1048" alt="Verification Results Awaiting Approval screen" />

## 5. What Happens After You Submit

Once you've completed your part, our team reviews the results - this typically takes a short while (~1 day for KYB, and can be quicker for KYC), though some cases may take longer if a manual review is required. We'll notify you of the outcome. Here's what the possible outcomes mean:

| Outcome | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Accepted | Your verification passed all checks. No further action is needed from you. |
| Under review | Your case has been sent for a manual compliance review. We may contact you if we need any additional information. |
| Action needed | Something in your submission couldn't be confirmed (for example, a document and selfie mismatch, or an expired ID). We'll reach out with next steps. |
| Link expired | The verification link wasn't completed in time. Simply let us know and we'll send you a fresh one. |

### Having trouble?

- **Link expired or not opening:** request a new link from your point of contact - links have a limited validity window.
- **Camera or liveness check not working:** move to a well-lit area, remove sunglasses/hats, and make sure your browser allows camera access.
- **Uploaded document not accepted:** check that it's not blurred, cropped, or expired, and that it's a supported document type for France.

## 6. Your Data & Privacy

Your documents and biometric data are processed securely by our verification partner, Shufti Pro, solely for the purpose of completing this verification. You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data at any time.

For any questions about your specific verification case, please reach out to your usual point of contact.
