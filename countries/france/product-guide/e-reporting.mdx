## Overview

E-Reporting is where your B2B international sales and purchases, and your B2C sales transactions, are reported to the PPF. These transactions are not exchanged over Peppol - there is no counterparty platform to send them to - but they still have to reach the tax authority, before the deadline for each reporting period based on your VAT regime.

Three things happen here:

- **You send.** Transactions arrive by manual file upload, API or SFTP, the same way your invoices do.
- **You track.** Each transaction carries a reporting status, telling you whether it has been reported to the PPF and what the outcome was.
- **You correct it.** Where a report fails, or the data you sent was wrong, this is where you can correct it.

E-Reporting appears on both Sales Documents and Purchase Documents, but the two sides carry different tabs. This chapter covers all of them and how to read each one.

Reporting payment has its own chapter after this one.

## How e-reporting differs from e-invoicing

E-invoicing documents move in real time once you submit them. ClearTax validates, delivers to your counterparty's platform, and their response comes back to you. E-reporting does not work that way.

There is no counterparty. Nothing is delivered to anyone's platform, nobody approves or disputes it, and there is no commercial lifecycle to follow. A report has one job: **to reach the PPF on time**.

Submission is not immediate either. You send your transactions to ClearTax whenever suits you, we hold them until the reporting window for your period closes, and we file them with the PPF on the deadline that applies to your VAT regime.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**You never trigger a submission.** There is no Submit button waiting on you and no deadline to diarise.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
**Sent to ClearTax is not the same as reported to the government.** The status tells you which of the two has happened.
</Callout>

## Submission timing and VAT regimes

You do not choose when your reports go to the PPF. ClearTax works it out from the VAT regime your entity is registered under, and files on time.

### When ClearTax files

**ClearTax files two days after your reporting period closes.**

Whatever your regime, the rule is the same. When a period ends, everything you have sent us for that period goes to the PPF two days later - well inside the mandate's deadline. This applies to your payment data as well as your transactions.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**The gap between when we file and when it is due is deliberate.** It leaves room to correct and resubmit anything the PPF rejects, without missing the deadline.
</Callout>

### Frequencies and deadlines by regime

How often a period closes, and when the mandate requires it to be filed.

| Your regime | Transaction data | Payment data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Real normal, monthly** | Three periods a month, filed 10 days after each one ends:<br />• days 1 to 10, due the 20th<br />• days 11 to 20, due the 30th<br />• day 21 to month end, due the 10th of the following month | Monthly, due before the 10th of the following month |
| **Real normal, quarterly** | Monthly, due before the 10th of the following month | Monthly, due before the 10th of the following month |
| **Simplified VAT** | Monthly, due between the 25th and 30th of the following month | Monthly, due between the 25th and 30th of the following month |
| **VAT exemption scheme** | Every two months, due between the 25th and 30th of the month after the period ends | Every two months, due between the 25th and 30th of the month after the period ends |

### What this means day to day

Nothing, most of the time. Send your transactions to ClearTax whenever suits your business - as they are raised, in a nightly batch, or once a week - and we hold them until your period closes.

A record sitting at **Pending** is usually not a problem. It means the period is still open, or the next scheduled run has not happened yet. It moves on its own.

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
**Send well before the deadline, not on it.** A transaction that fails validation still needs correcting and resubmitting, and that has to happen inside the same window.
</Callout>

From the e-Invoicing dashboard, open **Sales Documents** or **Purchase Documents** and click the **E-Reporting** tab. Sales Documents carry your exports and your consumer sales. Purchase Documents carries your imports.

## What every tab shares

Statuses, summary cards, filters and actions work the same way wherever you are. Only the columns change from tab to tab.

### The status info panel

Click **Learn more** above the table to open the status info panel, which lists every status along with its definition.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/product-guide/e-reporting/68-f66.png" width="2048" height="1176" alt="Status info panel" />

#### Reporting status

Every record carries a reporting status, showing its progress towards the PPF.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | ClearTax has received the transaction and queued it for submission. It will be transmitted in the next scheduled cycle. |
| **In Progress** | The transaction has been sent and is being processed. This is temporary - it moves to Reported or Failed once the PPF confirms. |
| **Reported** | Submitted to the PPF and accepted. Both the technical and content checks passed. |
| **Failed** | Something went wrong. Either ClearTax found a problem before sending, or the PPF rejected it afterwards. Open the invoice, review the error and correct it. |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**On the B2C Invoice tab, Reported is replaced by Consolidated.** Individual consumer invoices are not reported one by one - they are grouped into a consolidated document, and it is that document which goes to the PPF. Consolidated means your invoice has been included in one.
</Callout>

#### Payment reporting status

Where a transaction also needs its payment reported, it carries a second status, shown in its own column.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Collected and stored at ClearTax, but not yet reported against this transaction. It will be reported according to your regime. |
| **Partially Reported** | Some of the payment has been reported, and more is outstanding |
| **Payment Reported** | The full amount has been reported |
| **Failed** | Reporting the payment to the PPF failed. Review the error and report it again. |

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
**The two statuses move independently.** A transaction can be Reported while its payment is still Pending, so both need watching - see the chapter on reporting payment. Payment can only be collected once the transaction itself has been reported successfully.
</Callout>

### Summary cards

Four cards sit above the table, counting the records in the date range you have filtered to. Clicking one filters the table to that status.

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Total** | Every record ingested in the selected date range |
| **Pending** | Ingested but not yet sent - the period is still open, or the next run has not happened |
| **In Progress** | Sent to the PPF and awaiting final confirmation |
| **Failed** | Rejected by the PPF, or failed ClearTax validation. These need correcting. |
| **Reported** | Accepted by the PPF. Nothing further is needed. |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**Failed is the only card representing work sitting with you.** Pending and In Progress resolve on their own.
</Callout>

On the **B2C Invoice** tab the last card reads **Consolidated** rather than Reported, counting the invoices that have been grouped into a consolidated document.

### Filtering the list

Click **Filter** to open the filter panel. Filters are grouped into sections, and combine with each other - each one narrows what the last one left.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/product-guide/e-reporting/69-f67.png" width="2048" height="1176" alt="Filter panel" />

#### The date range is mandatory

Every e-reporting tab needs a date range before it will show you anything.

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
**Date Range** defaults to the last 30 days. If a transaction seems to be missing, widen or move the date range before assuming anything is wrong - this is the most common reason a record cannot be found.
</Callout>

Which date the range applies to depends on the tab:

| Tab | Date filtered on |
| --- | --- |
| **B2B International** | Document Date |
| **B2C Invoice** | Document Date |
| **B2C Consolidated** | Document Reporting Date |
| **B2C Consolidated Payments** | Payment Reporting Date |

#### Applying and clearing filters

At the bottom of the panel:

- **Apply** - applies your selections. The table refreshes, and each active filter appears as a chip above it.
- **Clear All** - resets every filter except the date range, which is required.

### Acting on documents

There are three things you can do to a record. Two of them start from the **Actions** dropdown above the table; editing happens inside the record itself.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/product-guide/e-reporting/70-f68.png" width="2048" height="1176" alt="Actions dropdown" />

| Action | Where you do it | Available on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Delete** | Actions dropdown | Records that have not yet been reported |
| **Confirm Payment** | Actions dropdown | Transactions that have been reported |
| **Edit** | Inside the detailed view | Records at Pending or Failed |

#### Deleting a record

Select the record, open **Actions** and choose **Delete**.

<Callout kind="warning" collapsed="false">
**You can only delete a record that has not been reported to the PPF.** Once a transaction has been reported it is on record with the tax authority, and removing it from ClearTax would not change that.
</Callout>

#### Editing a record

Editing is done from the **detailed view**, not the Actions dropdown. Open the record by clicking its document number, then correct it there.

It is available only while a record is at **Pending** or **Failed** - that is, before the PPF has accepted it. Once a record is Reported, editing is closed.

Saving a **Failed** record re-runs validation. If it passes, the record returns to **Pending** and joins the next scheduled submission.

#### Confirming payment

Where VAT is due on collection, the payment has to be reported. Select the transaction, open **Actions** and choose **Confirm Payment**.

ClearTax stores what you record and reports it to the PPF according to your regime - you are not filing at the moment you confirm.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**Payment can only be confirmed once the transaction has been reported.** Until then there is nothing for the payment to be reported against.
</Callout>

Confirming payment is covered in full in the chapter that follows.

## Reporting payment

Where VAT is due when you collect rather than when you invoice, the payment has to be reported as well as the transaction. This applies to your **B2B international sales** and your **B2C sales**.

The mechanics are the same as reporting payment on a domestic invoice, with one difference: **the date of collection is required here**.

Three things happen here:

- **You collect.** Money reaches your bank, in full or in part.
- **You confirm.** You record what you collected, split by VAT rate, against the transaction it relates to.
- **ClearTax reports it.** We store what you confirm and file it with the PPF on your regime's deadline, exactly as we do for transactions.

### Before you start

Three things are worth knowing before you open the modal.

| Rule | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Services only** | Payment reporting applies where VAT is due on collection. Goods are not reported this way. |
| **Report in EUR** | Payments are reported in euros, whatever currency the transaction was in. |
| **Split by VAT rate** | You record what you collected against each rate, not as a single figure. |

### How to confirm a payment

**1. Find the transaction** on the B2B International or B2C tab and select it.

**2. Open the Actions dropdown and choose Confirm Payment.**

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/product-guide/e-reporting/74-f72.png" width="2048" height="1176" alt="Confirm Payment modal" />

**3. Check the figures ClearTax has filled in.** The modal shows your customer, the invoice number and issue date, and how much of the invoice has been collected so far - the total, what is already collected, and what is still due.

**4. Enter the date of collection.** This is required. It is the date the money reached you, not the date you are recording it.

**5. Enter what you collected, split by VAT rate.** Each rate on the transaction is listed with the balance still due against it. Fill in what you collected for each. The total updates as you go.

**6. Click Save & Report Payment.** ClearTax stores the collection and reports it to the PPF on your regime's next deadline.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**Saving does not file immediately.** As with transactions, ClearTax holds what you confirm and submits it when your reporting period closes.
</Callout>

### Collecting in more than one payment

Payments often arrive in installments. Record each one as it lands.

After a partial collection the payment status becomes **Partially Reported**, and the modal remembers what you have already recorded - when you come back, the balance due reflects the earlier payment.

Once the full amount has been collected and recorded, the status becomes **Payment Reported**.

#### Payment history

Every collection you record is kept, so you can see what was reported and what happened to it.

Click **Payment History** on the transaction row to open the payment history drawer.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/france/product-guide/e-reporting/75-e73.png" width="2048" height="1176" alt="Payment history" />

The drawer lists each payment recorded against the transaction, what it consisted of, and where its reporting stands.

### B2C consolidated payments

Payments against consumer sales are not reported one by one. ClearTax groups them the same way it groups the invoices themselves, and reports them as a consolidated payment document for the period.

You confirm each collection as normal. The **B2C Consolidated Payments** tab shows the resulting documents - how many payments each covers, how many invoices they relate to, the VAT rate, and the total reported.

## When a report fails

A record at **Failed** was either stopped by ClearTax before it was sent, or rejected by the PPF after it arrived. Either way it has not been reported, and it will not be reported until you or ClearTax put it right.

Open the record to see the reason.

### Failures ClearTax resolves

Some rejections are about how the transmission was built rather than what was in it. You do not need to do anything - ClearTax rebuilds and resends.

| What went wrong | What ClearTax does |
| --- | --- |
| The transmission was too large | Splits it and resubmits |
| The transmission was a duplicate | Rebuilds it with a new reference |
| The transmission was named incorrectly | Corrects the name and resends |
| The file structure was invalid | Corrects it and resends |

### Failures you need to fix

Others are about the data you sent, and only you can correct them.

| What went wrong | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **A field is not valid** - for example a VAT number in the wrong format | Open the record, click **Edit**, correct the field and save. The record returns to Pending. |
| **Figures do not add up** - totals inconsistent with the lines | Open the record, click **Edit**, correct the amounts and save. |
| **Already reported** - the PPF has this transaction on record | You cannot report it again. |

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
**Fix a failure before the deadline, not on it.** A corrected record still has to be resubmitted and accepted, and that has to happen inside the same reporting window.
</Callout>
