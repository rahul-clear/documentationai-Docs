This product guide walks through the ClearTax France e-Invoicing and e-Reporting product end to end - from finding your way around the dashboard and importing documents, through sending and receiving invoices, handling buyer responses and exceptions, reporting payments and self-billed invoices, to e-reporting B2B international and B2C transactions.

France's e-invoicing mandate, run by the DGFiP, requires businesses to exchange invoices through a certified Plateforme Agréée (PA) such as ClearTax. Invoices are exchanged with your buyer's or supplier's PA over the Peppol network, and the mandatory data is reported to the PPF (Portail Public de Facturation).

| Detail | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Tax authority | DGFiP |
| Exchange | Between Plateformes Agréées (PA) over the Peppol network |
| Government reporting | PPF (Portail Public de Facturation) |
| Entity identifiers | SIREN (entity) and SIRET (branch) |
| Covers | B2B e-invoicing, self-billed invoices, and B2B international / B2C e-reporting |
