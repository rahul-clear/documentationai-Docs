France's e-invoicing mandate, run by the DGFiP, requires businesses to send and receive invoices through a certified Plateforme Agréée (PA) such as ClearTax, exchanging over the Peppol network and filing through the PPF.

This page covers how sales and purchase invoices flow through ClearTax (see [Flows](/countries/france/flows)), onboarding steps specific to France, and API reference material (see [Setup & Reference](/countries/france/setup-reference)).

## Onboarding specific to France

- **Business hierarchy setup** - SIREN legal entities, SIRET establishments, and routing codes configured so invoices route to the correct entity or branch.
- **Business and identity verification (KYB/KYC)** - ClearTax verifies legal existence and the legal representative's identity via official French and European registries, meeting French AML and eIDAS requirements for certified PAs.
- ClearTax registers the business with DGFiP as a linked entity under its PA accreditation; the SIREN is enrolled in the Annuaire, the French e-invoicing directory used to route invoices correctly.
- A demo environment is provided to test integrations end-to-end before going live in production.

## Regulatory basis

The mandate is grounded in the Loi de Finances 2020, operationalised by the DGFiP. From 1 September 2026, all companies must be able to receive e-invoices, and large/mid-sized companies must issue them via a registered Plateforme Agréée (PDP); small and micro-enterprises follow from 1 September 2027.

Source: [impots.gouv.fr - Facturation électronique](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/je-decouvre-la-facturation-electronique)
