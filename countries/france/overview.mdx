France's e-invoicing mandate, run by the DGFiP, requires businesses to send and receive invoices through a certified Plateforme Agréée (PA) such as ClearTax, exchanging over the Peppol network and filing through the PPF.

## Regulatory basis

The mandate is grounded in the Loi de Finances 2020, operationalised by the DGFiP. From 1 September 2026, all companies must be able to receive e-invoices, and large/mid-sized companies must issue them via a registered Plateforme Agréée (PDP); small and micro-enterprises follow from 1 September 2027.
