Before you can exchange e-invoices in Oman through ClearTax, you need to appoint ClearTax as your Accredited Service Provider (ASP) on the OTA Fawtara portal. This guide walks you through sending a connection request to ClearTax on Fawtara and explains what happens after you submit it.

## How it works

1. **You send a connection request** to ClearTax on the Fawtara portal.

2. **ClearTax accepts the request** on Fawtara.

3. **ClearTax creates your Peppol IDs** and links them to your ClearTax workspace.

## Timelines

The OTA sets the following SLAs for ASPs:

| **Step**                                               | **SLA**         |
|--------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Accepting your connection request                      | 1 business day  |
| Creating your Peppol IDs after the request is accepted | 3 business days |

## Send a connection request to ClearTax

### Step 1 - Log in to Fawtara

Log in to the [Fawtara portal](https://fawtara.taxoman.gov.om/) with your organisation's credentials.

### Step 2 - Open the ASP directory

Select **Accredited Service Providers** from the top navigation bar. This opens the list of all ASPs accredited in Oman.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/onboarding-via-fawtara/fawtara-portal-home-page-with-the-top-na.jpg" width="1400" height="636" alt="Fawtara portal home page with the top navigation bar" />

### Step 3 - Find ClearTax and connect

Search for ClearTax and click **Connect**. ClearTax is listed as:

- **Name:** DEFMACRO SOFTWARE

- **Solution Name:** ClearTax

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/onboarding-via-fawtara/cleartax-listing-with-the-connect-button.jpg" width="1400" height="658" alt="ClearTax listing with the Connect button" />

### Step 4 - Confirm your stage of association

A pop-up asks which stage you have reached with ClearTax. Select all four stages:

- Identified Service Provider

- Scope and Commercial Negotiation/Discussion

- Scope and Commercials Finalized

- Agreement/Contract Signed

All four stages must be selected before you can continue. Click **Proceed**.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/onboarding-via-fawtara/stage-of-association-pop-up.jpg" width="1400" height="659" alt="Stage of association pop-up" />

### Step 5 - Leave the Effective Date blank

You can optionally enter an **Effective Date** (onboarding date). We recommend leaving it blank: the connection then takes effect as soon as ClearTax accepts your request, and your Peppol IDs can be created straight away. If you set a future date, Peppol ID creation waits until that date.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/onboarding-via-fawtara/connect-to-service-provider-dialog-with-.jpg" width="1400" height="872" alt="Connect To Service Provider dialog with the optional Effective Date" />

### Step 6 - Confirm and submit

A pop-up asks if you want to proceed. Click **Yes**. A confirmation appears showing that your connection request has been submitted.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/onboarding-via-fawtara/confirmation-pop-up-click-yes-to-proceed.jpg" width="1400" height="619" alt="Confirmation pop-up: click Yes to proceed" />

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rahul-clear/documentationai-Docs/main/images/countries/oman/onboarding-via-fawtara/submission-completed.jpg" width="1400" height="616" alt="Submission completed" />

## What happens next

1. **ClearTax accepts your request** on Fawtara within 1 business day.

2. **ClearTax registers your VAT numbers** on the Oman Peppol network (sandbox and/or production) and links them to your ClearTax workspace. This is done within 3 business days of acceptance. If you have more than one entity, a Peppol ID is created for each VAT number.

3. **Your Customer Success Manager confirms** once your Peppol IDs are live.

You can then start exchanging e-invoices through ClearTax.

## Error scenarios and troubleshooting

| **Issue**                                                     | **What it means**                                      | **Resolution**                                                                                                            |
|---------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The **Proceed** button is not available on the stage pop-up   | Not all four stages of association are selected        | Select all four stages, including **Agreement/Contract Signed**, then click **Proceed**.                                  |
| The connection is accepted but Peppol IDs are not yet created | An Effective Date in the future was set on the request | Peppol IDs are created once the Effective Date is reached. Contact your CSM if you need to bring this forward.            |
| No update after the SLA has passed                            | The request may be pending on Fawtara or with ClearTax | Check the request status on Fawtara, then contact your CSM or the ClearTax Support Desk with your company name and VATIN. |
