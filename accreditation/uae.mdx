---
title: Accreditation - UAE
description: ClearTax's UAE eInvoicing Office accreditation status
---
ClearTax (Defmacro Software DMCC) is officially accredited as an E-Invoicing Accredited Service Provider (ASP) by the UAE's eInvoicing Office, ranking among the UAE's top 5 accredited ASPs. Full accreditation was granted after review and approval of ClearTax's Production Testing documentation, confirming readiness to provide compliant electronic invoicing services within the UAE.
