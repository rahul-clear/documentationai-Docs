description: "ClearTax's Oman Tax Authority (OTA) Fawtara accreditation status"

ClearTax (Defmacro Software SPC) is officially accredited by the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) as an e-invoicing Service Provider, listed on the Fawtara Portal's public directory of Accredited Service Providers. This confirms ClearTax's readiness to support Omani enterprises through their e-invoicing compliance journey under the Fawtara mandate.
