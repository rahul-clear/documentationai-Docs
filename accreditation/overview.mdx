## What is accreditation? And why is it important?

**What it means:**

- A tax authority (or its delegated regulator) formally approves a service provider's systems as meeting the country's technical, security, and compliance requirements for issuing, exchanging, or reporting e-invoices.
- Depending on the mandate, this approval goes by different names: Accredited Service Provider (ASP) status, a certified Peppol Access Point, or a Plateforme Agréée (PA).
- Providers earn it by passing the authority's testing, security, and data-handling checks before going live.

**Why it matters:**

- **It's usually a legal requirement** - most e-invoicing mandates only allow accredited providers to exchange or report invoices on your behalf.
- **It's proof you can trust the provider** - accreditation status by country is a direct, verifiable answer to "is this provider actually authorised to operate in my market?"
