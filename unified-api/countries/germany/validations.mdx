### Billing

**Core Invoice Rules**
- **BR-08**: An Invoice shall contain the Seller postal address.
- **BR-10**: An Invoice shall contain the Buyer postal address (BG-8).
- **BR-16**: An Invoice shall have at least one Invoice line (BG-25).
- **BR-19**: Seller tax representative postal address required if representative exists.

**VAT & Tax Rules (BR-S, BR-Z, BR-E, BR-O, BR-G, BR-IC, BR-AE, BR-AF, BR-AG, BR-B series)**
Rules governing Standard rated, Zero rated, Exempt, Not subject to VAT, Export outside EU, Intra-community supply, Reverse charge, IGIC, and IPSI categories — each with requirements for VAT category codes, VAT rates (0 or greater than zero), Seller/Buyer VAT identifiers, and VAT breakdown calculations.

**Payment & Financial Rules**
- **BR-50, BR-61**: Payment account identifier required for credit transfers.
- **BR-51**: Card primary account number (BT-87) shall show maximum 6+4 digits.
- **BR-66, BR-67**: Maximum one payment card and one mandate per invoice.

**Code List Rules (BR-CL series)**: Standards for coding document types, currencies, countries, tax categories, allowances, charges, items, endpoints, and units.

**UBL Constraint Rules (UBL-CR series)**: 600+ rules specifying elements that should not be included in UBL invoices (extensions, version IDs, various party/delivery/payment attributes).
