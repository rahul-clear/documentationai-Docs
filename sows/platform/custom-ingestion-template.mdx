---
title: "Custom Ingestion Template"
description: "Self-service field mapping for custom invoice ingestion formats"
---

- Your team can create a custom ingestion template directly through workspace settings.
- The platform automatically maps standard recognised fields, with a self-service interface for your team to manually map any remaining custom fields.
- Template configurations can be categorised and filtered by business parameters, including business entity or TRN/TIN.
- This is a self-serve tool - your team can independently manage, validate, and update template configurations without needing ongoing custom development or manual technical intervention.
- Saved templates also appear in the relevant modules during ingestion.
