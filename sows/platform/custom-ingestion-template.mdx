---
title: Custom Ingestion Template
description: Self-service field mapping for custom invoice ingestion formats
---
✓ Provide the ability to create a custom template

✓ The platform will automatically perform 1:1 mapping for standard recognized fields while providing an intuitive self-service interface to manually map remaining custom fields.

✓ Template configurations can be categorized and filtered based on specific business parameters, including Business Entity or Tax Identifier.

✓ This is a self-serve tool where your team can independently manage, validate, and update template configurations without requiring ongoing custom software development or manual technical intervention.

✓ The saved templates will also appear in the individual modules during ingestion.&#x20;
