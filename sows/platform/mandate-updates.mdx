---
title: Updates in Mandate
description: Government-mandated regulatory updates delivered in the core product
---
*Countries applicable: All*

✓ Government-mandated updates will be integrated directly into the core product at no extra charge.

✓ Clients will receive email notifications regarding regulatory modifications within 5 business days of official publication by government authorities.

✓ Updated API documentation and UI guidelines will be provided alongside recommended timelines to assist clients in adjusting their internal ERP, AP, or accounting software systems.

✓ Note: Development effort and costs for client-side system adjustments are excluded from this scope and remain the client's responsibility (typically managed via internal IT or third-party system integrators).
