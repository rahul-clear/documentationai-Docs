## Sales Reconciliation

- Lets taxpayers reconcile their sales invoice data in their ERP against the records available on the ClearTax platform.
- Taxpayers upload their sales data from their ERP or source system in ClearTax's standard template format for comparison.
- The platform identifies mismatches between the uploaded data and invoices processed by ClearTax, including missing invoices, amount differences, and status discrepancies.
- A summary report is generated for each reconciliation run, showing matched records, mismatches, and invoices pending tax-authority acknowledgement.
- The reconciliation report can be downloaded in Excel format for return preparation and internal audit purposes.
- Reconciliation can be triggered on demand or run automatically at the close of each filing period.
- Identified mismatches can be reviewed and marked as resolved directly on the platform.

## GL Powered VAT Automation with Recon

- Connects General Ledger (GL) data from the taxpayer's ERP to the ClearTax platform, so invoice data can be automatically extracted and matched against GL entries.
- VAT calculations are validated automatically by comparing tax amounts in the GL against tax-authority-validated invoices, removing the need for manual cross-checks.
- The platform identifies discrepancies between the GL and validated invoice data, including differences in taxable amounts, VAT amounts, and invoice counts for a given period.
- A reconciliation report shows matched entries, mismatches, and any invoices present in the GL but missing from tax-authority records (or vice versa).
- The report is mapped to the relevant VAT return line items, helping finance teams prepare and verify their VAT return filing with confidence.
- Reconciliation runs automatically on a schedule aligned to the filing cycle, and can also be triggered on demand.
- Mismatches can be reviewed, annotated, and resolved directly on the platform, with a full audit trail of actions taken.

## Reports

- Your team can download reports of invoices processed through ClearTax in Excel format for internal reporting, audit, and analysis.
- **Lite report** - a summary of invoices with key fields: invoice number, date, counterparty, amount, tax, and lifecycle status.
- **Detailed report** - a full field-level extract including invoice header, party, line-item, tax, and lifecycle data.
- Reports can be generated on demand from the ClearTax platform.

## MIS Dashboard

A real-time management dashboard on the ClearTax platform, giving visual summaries of invoice activity - volumes, values, statuses, and trends - for finance, operations, and compliance monitoring.

- **Volume metrics** - total invoices processed, direction (sales/purchase), time period, etc.
- **Value metrics** - aggregate invoice amounts, taxable amounts, and tax amounts.
- **Status metrics** - distribution of invoices across lifecycle stages.
- Separate views are available for sales invoices (issued by your business) and purchase invoices (received from suppliers).
- Data on the dashboard updates in near-real-time as invoices move through the platform.

## XML Download

- Your team can download the XML files transmitted from the ClearTax platform to the counterparty's Access Point and to the tax authority, giving a verifiable record of exactly what was sent for audit and regulatory review.
- Available downloads per invoice typically include the invoice XML sent to the counterparty's Access Point, the tax data document submitted to the authority, and the MLS/response XMLs received for each exchange.
- XML files can be downloaded per invoice from the ClearTax platform.
- Downloaded files are the exact payloads transmitted, unchanged after transmission.
