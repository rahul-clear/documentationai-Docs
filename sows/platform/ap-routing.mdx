---
title: Auto AP Mailbox routing
description: Automated delivery of received invoices into your AP automation inbox
---
\
✓ ClearTax auto delivers received invoices to an AP inbox (s) predefined by the customer.

- Customers can also request to define Business Rules for routing documents to different email inboxes.
- The recipient email address, sender name, and subject-line format are configured during onboarding.
- Each delivery includes the invoice PDF as email attachment. XML attachment is available on request with an OTP.

✓ Delivery failures are retried per ClearTax's standard email delivery policy.

✓ Delivery status is visible on your dashboard and audit trail.
