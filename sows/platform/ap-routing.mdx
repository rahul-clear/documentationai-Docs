### Auto AP Mailbox routing (add-on SKU):

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> ClearTax auto delivers received invoices to an AP inbox (s) predefined by the customer.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Customers can also request to define Business Rules for routing documents to different email inboxes.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> The recipient email address, sender name, and subject-line format are configured during onboarding.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Each delivery includes the invoice PDF as email attachment. XML attachment is available on request with an OTP.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Delivery failures are retried per ClearTax's standard email delivery policy.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Delivery status is visible on your dashboard and audit trail.
