---
title: Data Archival and Storage
description: 'Cloud storage, retention tiers, and download policy for invoice data'
---
*Countries applicable: All*

Taxpayers are provided archival storage of eInvoice data exchanged as per the government mandated guidelines. E.g. 10 years in UAE & Oman, 10 years in France

✓ Short Term data retrieval

-  Active use - documents where the difference between the document date and current date is less than 90 days will be available for active use, viewable in the 'Documents' view with all actions supported.

✓ Long Term/Infrequent use

-  Documents (XMLs, JSONs, etc. i.e. the same format which has been transmitted to counterparty or govt endpoints) between 90 and 365 days old will be available via the 'Archives' page through reports and PDF download or can be requested for download via CSM.
- Ability to download human readable PDFs and Audit Trails for long term data i.e. greater than 90 days is available as a separate SKU with OTP.

✓ All invoice data beyond the retention period will be purged from ClearTax databases and will be unavailable for any use. Your team is required to download the data before the end of the retention period and store it on your systems.
