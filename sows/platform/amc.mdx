✓ The Annual Maintenance Charge (AMC) covers ongoing platform support and maintenance services for the FTA e-invoicing solution post go-live.

✓ AMC includes all regulatory updates required to keep the platform compliant with the government mandate changes, schema updates, and any new compliance requirements issued during the contract period.

✓ Bug fixes and patches for issues arising from the production environment are covered under AMC. AMC includes access to ClearTax support channels with agreed SLA-backed response times for issue resolution.

✓ Platform monitoring, uptime management, and infrastructure maintenance for the UAE environment are included.

✓ AMC does not cover new feature development, additional integrations, or changes driven by your internal systems; these would be scoped and priced separately.

✓ Responses to queries and clarifications related to the deployed e-invoicing solution are included under AMC.
