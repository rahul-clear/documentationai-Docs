### Invoice Print Template (add-on SKU):

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Taxpayers can choose from pre-configured Tax Authority compliant invoice print templates available on the ClearTax platform.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> All templates support both Tax Invoices and Simplified Tax Invoices, including associated credit and debit notes.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Invoice PDFs are generated in English and geo-specific language (e.g. Arabic for KSA, UAE, Oman, French, German etc. for EU).

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Taxpayers can also set a default template for their workspace which will be applied to all print requests, with a preview available before setting it as default.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Bulk printing of invoices is supported, with output delivered as a downloadable file.

### Custom Ingestion Template:

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Provide the ability to create a custom template

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> The platform will automatically perform 1:1 mapping for standard recognized fields while providing an intuitive self-service interface to manually map remaining custom fields.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Template configurations can be categorized and filtered based on specific business parameters, including Business Entity or Tax Identifier.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> This is a self-serve tool where your team can independently manage, validate, and update template configurations without requiring ongoing custom software development or manual technical intervention.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> The saved templates will also appear in the individual modules during ingestion.
