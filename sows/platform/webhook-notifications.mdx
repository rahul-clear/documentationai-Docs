### Webhook Notifications (add-on SKU):

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Webhooks are currently available for UAE, Oman and France.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Real-time notifications pushed to your systems (pre-set endpoint) as e-invoicing events occur.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Event types published to you include invoice lifecycle changes, C5 reporting outcomes, MLS delivery status updates, and buyer acknowledgement confirmations.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Delivery and security - events are delivered securely to an endpoint you provide. Full technical specifications, endpoint requirements, and integration steps are provided in the ClearTax webhook setup guide, shared with your IT team during onboarding.
