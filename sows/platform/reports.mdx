---
title: Reports
description: >-
  Download reports of invoices processed through the ClearTax platform in Excel
  format for internal reporting, audit, and analysis purposes.
---
✓ Lite report - a summary of invoices with key fields such as invoice number, date, buyer, amount, tax, and lifecycle status.

✓ Detailed report - a full field-level extract including invoice header, party, line-item, tax, and lifecycle data.

✓ Reports can be generated on demand from the ClearTax platform UI.
