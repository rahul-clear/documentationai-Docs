### Reports:

Download reports of invoices processed through the ClearTax platform in Excel format for internal reporting, audit, and analysis purposes.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Lite report - a summary of invoices with key fields such as invoice number, date, buyer, amount, tax, and lifecycle status.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Detailed report - a full field-level extract including invoice header, party, line-item, tax, and lifecycle data.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Reports can be generated on demand from the ClearTax platform UI.
