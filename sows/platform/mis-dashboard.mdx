### MIS Dashboard:

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Real-time analytics dashboard on the ClearTax platform, providing visual summaries of invoice activity - volumes, values, statuses, and trends - for finance, operations, and compliance monitoring.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Volume metrics - total invoices processed, direction (sales / purchase), time period etc.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Value metrics - aggregate invoice amounts, taxable amounts, and VAT amounts etc.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Status metrics - distribution of invoices across lifecycle stages.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Views are available currently for Sales flow (Supplier side) only. In near future dashboard for Purchase flow (for applicable geos) will also be released. Will be communicated via CSM/Product Update Emails.
