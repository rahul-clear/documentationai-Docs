### Send document via Email:

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> The feature can be used to email Tax Invoices, Simplified Tax Invoices and their associated credit/debit notes. Documents are attached as PDFs.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> As a Seller: Emails can be manually or auto-sent once the e-Invoice reaches the buyer network (Peppol).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> As a Buyer: Emails can be manually forwarded once the e-Invoice is received via the Peppol network.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> The email is triggered from ClearTax mailer services by default; the email server can be changed to your own server on demand, requiring a one-time setup and configuration.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> The email content is predefined as a generic message, with support for modification and multiple templates across different invoice types on request.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> Emails are sent in English (support for other languages can be requested via your ClearTax account manager)
