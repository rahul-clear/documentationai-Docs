---
title: "Email"
description: "Email delivery for e-invoices, credit/debit notes, and payment reminders"
---

- Used to email Tax Invoices, Simplified Tax Invoices, and their associated credit/debit notes, with a link to download invoice PDFs or attachments.
- An email API can trigger emails of e-invoices and payment reminders for single or multiple documents.
- Taxpayers can also use the web portal's email functionality to send e-invoices to their buyers.
- Emails are triggered from ClearTax mailer services by default; the email server can be switched to the taxpayer's own server via a one-time setup.
- Email content is predefined but customizable, including support for multiple templates per invoice type.
- The reply-to email address and contacts can be configured.
- Emails are sent in the recipient's preferred language where supported.

<Callout kind="info">
Availability varies by country, including Belgium and Croatia.
</Callout>
