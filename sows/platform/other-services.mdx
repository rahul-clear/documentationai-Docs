## Email

- Used to email Tax Invoices, Simplified Tax Invoices, and their associated credit/debit notes, with a link to download invoice PDFs or attachments.
- An email API can trigger emails of e-invoices and payment reminders for single or multiple documents.
- Taxpayers can also use the web portal's email functionality to send e-invoices to their buyers.
- Emails are triggered from ClearTax mailer services by default; the email server can be switched to the taxpayer's own server via a one-time setup.
- Email content is predefined but customizable, including support for multiple templates per invoice type.
- The reply-to email address and contacts can be configured.
- Emails are sent in the recipient's preferred language where supported.

<Callout kind="info">
Availability varies by country, including Belgium and Croatia.
</Callout>

## Customer Print Template

- Taxpayers can choose from multiple pre-configured, compliant invoice print templates on the ClearTax platform.
- Templates support Tax Invoices and Simplified Tax Invoices, including associated credit and debit notes.
- Invoice PDFs can be generated in multiple languages depending on market requirements.
- Taxpayers can set a default template for their workspace, applied to all print requests, with a preview available before setting it as default.
- Bulk printing of invoices is supported, with output delivered as a downloadable file.
- Additional custom print templates can be requested separately - reach out to your ClearTax account team for scoping and pricing.

## Data Archival and Storage

- Taxpayers can opt for storage of invoices on ClearTax's cloud as per their requirements; data is purged once the opted storage period ends.
- All invoice data is available for download and use via reports and PDFs during the storage period.
- Taxpayers are required to download their data before the storage period completes, as data beyond that period is purged and unavailable for further use.
- Data is tiered by age for performance:
  - **Active use** - documents within the last 90 days are fully available in the Documents view for all actions.
  - **Infrequent use** - documents between 90-365 days old are accessible from the Archives page via reports and PDF download.
  - **Archived** - documents older than 365 days are accessible from the Archives page via reports and PDF download (typically takes several hours depending on data size).

## Annual Maintenance Charge (AMC)

- Covers ongoing platform support and maintenance for the e-invoicing solution post go-live.
- Includes regulatory updates required to keep the platform compliant with mandate changes, schema updates, and new compliance requirements during the contract period.
- Bug fixes and patches for issues arising in the production environment are covered.
- Includes access to ClearTax support channels with agreed SLA-backed response times.
- Platform monitoring, uptime management, and infrastructure maintenance are included.
- Does not cover new feature development, additional integrations, or changes driven by the taxpayer's internal systems - these are scoped and priced separately.
- Responses to queries and clarifications related to the deployed solution are included.

## License

- Grants access to the ClearTax e-invoicing platform to generate and submit compliant Tax Invoices and Simplified Tax Invoices, including associated credit and debit notes.
- Valid for the contracted period and covers a defined annual invoice volume per the selected plan.
- Invoice generation is restricted once the contracted volume is consumed; taxpayers are notified in advance at usage thresholds.
- Workspace-specific, covering the number of workspaces per the selected plan.
- A usage dashboard shows invoices generated, remaining volume, and license validity period.
- License renewal and volume upgrades can be requested and take effect from the date of the updated contract.
