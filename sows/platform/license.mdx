---
title: "License"
description: "Platform access, contracted invoice volume, and workspace coverage"
---

- Grants access to the ClearTax e-invoicing platform to generate and submit compliant Tax Invoices and Simplified Tax Invoices, including associated credit and debit notes.
- Valid for the contracted period and covers a defined annual invoice volume per the selected plan.
- Invoice generation is restricted once the contracted volume is consumed; taxpayers are notified in advance at usage thresholds.
- Workspace-specific, covering the number of workspaces per the selected plan.
- A usage dashboard shows invoices generated, remaining volume, and license validity period.
- License renewal and volume upgrades can be requested and take effect from the date of the updated contract.
