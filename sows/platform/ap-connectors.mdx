---
title: AP Connectors
description: Add-on to deliver received invoices into Ariba or Coupa via connectors
---
*Countries applicable: Only in C4 & C5 Peppol countries*

✓ ClearTax auto "lands" received invoices to an Ariba or Coupa via connectors (includes transformation on cXML format)

- Customers can also request to define Business Rules for routing documents to different destinations.

✓ Delivery status is visible on your dashboard and audit trail.
