---
title: SFTP Lifecycle Support
description: Add-on to capture seller- and buyer-side eInvoice lifecycle events via SFTP
---
*Countries applicable: Only in C4 & C5 Peppol countries*

✓ Support for capturing eInvoice lifecycle events from Seller (or Supplier) side. E.g. Payment reporting, Partial Payment reporting (*in France, Croatia, upcoming - Spain*)

✓ Support for capturing eInvoice lifecycle events from Buyer (Receiver) side E.g. (Refused, Disputed, Approved etc.)
