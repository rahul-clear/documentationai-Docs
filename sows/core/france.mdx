Access to the ClearTax France e-invoicing platform to send, receive, and report invoices via ClearTax's certified Plateforme Agréée (PA), keeping you compliant with the DGFiP e-invoicing mandate.

**Regulatory basis** — The mandate is grounded in the Loi de Finances 2020, operationalised by the DGFiP. From 1 September 2026, all companies must be able to receive e-invoices, and large/mid-sized companies must issue them via a registered Plateforme Agréée (PDP); small and micro-enterprises follow from 1 September 2027. Source: [impots.gouv.fr - Facturation électronique](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/professionnel/je-decouvre-la-facturation-electronique)

## Scope

1. **Onboarding** — your SIREN is registered in the Annuaire, the French e-invoicing directory, so buyers and suppliers can route invoices to you
2. **Type of transactions covered (AP and AR side)**
   1. B2B (domestic, via the PPF)
   2. B2C and cross-border transactions (via e-reporting)
3. **Type of documents covered** — all document types recognised under the French mandate (accepted in UBL, CII, or Factur-X on receipt), plus self-billing variants under formal mandate agreements (UC19b)
4. **Govt reporting** — every status change the mandate requires reporting on is filed with the PPF within a 24-hour window; e-reporting covers cross-border sales/purchases, B2C transactions, and related payment reporting
5. **Lifecycle management** — manage the full lifecycle of purchase and sales invoices as defined by the mandate, with all required actions reported to DGFiP
6. **SLAs** — 24-hour filing window with the PPF for mandate-required status changes
7. **RBAC** — role-based access control lets your workspace administrator assign users to roles with different permissions, controlling who can view, create, approve, or act on invoices within your workspace, in line with your internal segregation-of-duties requirements

## Channels supported

1. **API** — REST endpoints for sending invoices, checking status, querying documents, performing lifecycle actions, and uploading e-reporting transactions
2. **Manual Ingestion** — direct upload via the ClearTax web platform
3. **SFTP** — standard Excel or CSV files

- **AFNOR business scenarios** — ClearTax supports 35/44 business scenarios defined in AFNOR XP Z12-014, with coverage expanding on an ongoing basis.
