Access to the ClearTax Poland e-invoicing platform to send, receive, and report invoices via Poland's National e-Invoice System (Krajowy System e-Faktur, KSeF), keeping you compliant with the Ministry of Finance's (MoF) e-invoicing mandate.

**Regulatory basis** — KSeF is overseen by the Ministry of Finance and becomes mandatory in phases: 1 February 2026 for large entities, 1 April 2026 for other entities, and 1 January 2027 for micro-enterprises. Source: [ksef.podatki.gov.pl](https://ksef.podatki.gov.pl/co-to-jest-ksef/)

## Scope

1. **Onboarding** — your Tax Identification Number (VAT) is registered and authenticated within KSeF, including configuration of authorised representatives
2. **Type of transactions covered (AP and AR side)**
   1. B2B (standard VAT invoices, including corrective invoices)
3. **Type of documents covered** — standard VAT invoices and corrective invoices under the FA(3) logical structure, with coverage expanding as the Ministry of Finance issues further schema variants
4. **Govt reporting** — every submission session and its outcome (accepted, rejected, or pending) is tracked, and the KSeF number — the official confirmation of receipt — is retrieved and stored against each invoice
5. **Lifecycle management** — manage the full lifecycle of purchase and sales invoices as defined by the KSeF mandate, including issuance of corrective invoices; all actions are reflected in KSeF as required
6. **SLAs** — KSeF session and status tracked per submission (accepted/rejected/pending)
7. **RBAC** — role-based access control lets your workspace administrator assign users to roles with different permissions, controlling who can view, create, approve, or act on invoices within your workspace, in line with your internal segregation-of-duties requirements

## Channels supported

1. **API** — REST endpoints for sending invoices, checking KSeF processing status, querying documents, getting PDFs, and receiving invoices
2. **Manual Ingestion** — direct upload via the ClearTax web platform
3. **SFTP** — standard Excel or CSV files
