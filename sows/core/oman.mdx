### 1. Tax Authority On-boarding

Customer starts on ClearTax and completes the ASP association on the Fawtara Portal:

1. **Connect on Fawtara Portal** - customer logs in to Fawtara, opens the Accredited Service Providers list, selects ClearTax, records the engagement stage (Identify SP → Scope & Commercial Negotiations → Finalized → Agreement Signed), sets the effective date, and submits the connection request
2. **ClearTax accepts on Fawtara** - ClearTax reviews and accepts the request via the Manage Taxpayers dashboard; customer is notified via email, SMS, on-screen, and portal
3. **SMP registration** - ClearTax adds the participant to Oman SMP within 3 business days (validated by Fawtara before Approve); SMP pushes the update to SML.

### 2. Off-boarding

Disconnection can be initiated by either party on the Fawtara Portal:

1. **Customer-initiated** - customer opens Manage Service Provider on Fawtara, clicks Disconnect, provides a reason, and submits
2. **ClearTax-initiated** - ClearTax opens Manage Taxpayers on Fawtara, selects Disconnect with reason; the customer has 3 days to Approve or Reject (no action = auto-cancelled)
3. **Withdrawal** - either party can withdraw a pending disconnection request before it is actioned
4. **SMP cleanup** - on successful disconnection, ClearTax has 1 business day to remove the participant from Oman SMP
5. **Data handover** - in-flight invoices and pending TDDs are drained; archived documents remain accessible for the statutory retention period
6. **Access revocation** - API keys, SFTP, and UI access are revoked on ClearTax

### 6. Accounts Receivable (ClearTax as C2 - seller side)

Customer sends eInvoices to buyers:

1. ClearTax ingests invoice data via API, UI, or SFTP
2. Validates data, enriches it, generates UUID
3. Looks up buyer's Access Point via Oman's central SMP (SML)
4. Delivers the invoice to the buyer's AP over AS4
5. Captures buyer's MLS response and shows status to the seller

### 7. Accounts Payable (ClearTax as C3 - buyer side)

Customer receives eInvoices from suppliers:

1. Accepts inbound invoices from the seller's AP
2. Validates against PINT-OM schema and business rules
3. Sends MLS acknowledgement back to the seller's AP
4. Stores and shares the document via API, UI, and SFTP
5. Supports accept/reject and PO reconciliation

### 8. Government Reporting (OTA - via TDD)

1. Every exchange is reported to OTA as a TDD, on both seller and buyer sides, within the defined SLA
2. If the buyer sends MLS = RE (rejection), ClearTax withdraws the TDD from FTA and notifies the seller
3. Retry and resubmission of TDDs on failure or timeout

### 9. SMP Management

ClearTax manages participants on Oman SMP via API:

1. **Add / Amend / Remove** - participant management API calls to Oman SMP
2. **Fawtara validation** - every SMP action is validated against Fawtara for an active SP–Taxpayer connection; Approve → applied, Reject → no change
3. **SML sync** - Oman SMP pushes updates to SML to keep participant metadata consistent across the network

### 10. Statuses

**AR (Seller-side)**

- Pending - queued, awaiting submission
- Submitted to exchange network - handed off to Peppol
- Delivered to buyer network - reached buyer's AP (C3)
- Delivered to buyer - reached buyer's ERP (C4)
- Failed - validation or delivery failed

**AP (Buyer-side)**

- Received - invoice arrived from supplier
- Failed - validation failed on receipt

**Government Reporting (FTA)**

- Not Submitted - TDD not yet dispatched
- Processing - TDD sent, awaiting response
- Accepted - FTA accepted the TDD
- Withdrawn - TDD withdrawn (following buyer RE)

**MLS response codes**

- AP - Accepted
- AB - Accepted with objection
- RE - Rejected (with reason code: SV syntax / BV business rule / BW warning / FD fraud)

### 11. B2C QR Code Generation

Oman's mandate requires every B2C sale to be accompanied by a human-readable receipt carrying a QR code that ties it back to the invoice reported to the OTA.

ClearTax supports two deployment modes for QR generation and reporting, both producing identical OTA-compliant output:

- **Online (cloud)** - your POS or ERP sends each invoice to ClearTax over API/SFTP; ClearTax generates the Seller UUID and QR code, returns them for printing, and reports the invoice to the OTA in the background. Best suited for modern ERP/e-commerce setups with reliable connectivity and enterprise-range invoice volumes.
- **Offline (SDK)** - ClearTax distributes a Java SDK deployed on your POS terminals. The SDK generates the QR code and Seller UUID locally with no network call required; invoice XMLs are queued locally and submitted to the OTA whenever the device next reaches connectivity. Best suited for high-volume, multi-store retail (50+ POS terminals, unreliable connectivity, or >1M invoices/month) where checkout latency cannot be tolerated.

A single seller can mix both modes - for example, SDK at remote retail stores and cloud API at head office/e-commerce.

ClearTax's SDK/API encapsulates the full QR encoding logic - TLV tag ordering, UTF-8 byte handling, and UUIDv5 hashing against the OTA namespace - so your systems never need to implement this directly, avoiding a known source of compliance failure.

### 12. SDK SoW:

- For high-volume, multi-store retail deployments with non-reliable internet connection, ClearTax provides a self-contained, offline-first Java SDK that generates OTA/PINT-OM-compliant e-invoices, XML, Seller UUID, and QR code → locally at the point of sale, with no network call required for the transaction itself.\

- Scope and responsibilities of ClearTax:
  - JSON-to-UBL conversion → the SDK converts JSON invoice data of a sale into the official PINT-OM UBL XML invoice format.
  - Rule-based validation → every invoice is validated against the defined OTA/PINT-OM business rule set
  - Seller UUID + QR generation → the SDK derives a deterministic Seller UUID and generates the QR code (Base64-encoded) for the printed receipt.
  - Fully offline transaction path → invoice xml generation, validation, and QR/UUID derivation run entirely offline without any internet connection
  - Embedded deployment model → The sdk.jar application will reside on the POS/KIOSK, on its Java classpath and will be called in-process per sale; validation rules are available by default and cached in memory for the life of the process, keeping per-sale latency low.
  - CLI tooling for QA, support, and batch work → the same jar ships a command-line interface for testing POS releases, investigating any disputed invoices and pulling activity logs.
  - Device onboarding / activation → each POS terminal/device is one-time activated against the ClearTax portal using the VATIN number, activation code, and auth token issued for that store/device; an un-onboarded or revoked device will be prevented from issuing invoices compliant.
  - Local activity logs → the SDK maintains local logs of device activity, exportable/clearable via the CLI, to support audits and debugging issues without disrupting the live POS invoicing process.\

- Deployment prerequisites:
  - Java 21 or newer on each device; the jar bundles everything else it needs → no separate install step.
  - A VAT number, activation code, and auth token per device/store, issued by ClearTax as part of onboarding.
  - Invoice data supplied per the SDK's JSON contract.
  - The machine should have minimum of 2 GB storage and a minimum processing capacity of 512 MB RAM but it is recommended that the system has 8 GB storage and 2 GB RAM for best performance
    - Storage will be used to store generated E-Invoice XMLs and log files
    - Processing would be used to sign XMLs and validate data\

- Client responsibilities:
  - **Integration**: Embedding sdk.jar into the POS/KIOSK/relevant channel application(s) and map sale data into the agreed JSON contract, sending all fields required by PINT-OM in the format defined by ClearTax.
  - **Invoice completeness**: Submit every in-scope sale document and its associated notes (credit/debit).
  - **Onboarding gate**: Ensuring each device is onboarded before it is permitted to issue live invoices. Any device that is not onboarded will not be able to issue compliant invoices
  - **Runtime maintenance**: Maintaining Java 21+ runtime on each device throughout the SDK’s operational life.
  - **SDK version currency**: Apply Cleartax-published SDK updates within the timelines communicated for each release so that devices remain aligned with the latest OTA/PINT-OM version.
  - **Tamper protection:** Do not tamper with the sdk or its logs. Any modification, tampering or deletion of the SDK and associated files may lead to non-compliance
  - **Systems not having ClearTax SDK**: In case a Customer is maintaining separate Point of Sale (PoS) systems which are not connected with ClearTax then the Customer must be solely responsible for generating and reporting E-invoices for documents generated from such systems.
  - **Connectivity for Reporting**: Ensure connectivity is sufficient for locally queued invoices to reach Cleartax (C2) within the OTA mandated reporting window of 24 hours via the strategy convenient to the business.
    - Reporting may originate directly from the POS device once it is online or via a back-office system that collects queued invoices from devices and relays them to Cleartax.
    - You can choose and manage either of these options to ensure compliance within the applicable mandate window.\

- Additional Dependencies:
  - **_End-to-end submission of the locally queued invoice XML to the OTA's central system (via API/SFTP leg) → this is a background process distinct from local generation and is scoped separately from the SDK's offline generation path._**
