## **Scope:**

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Peppol network exchange** - ClearTax operates as your registered Peppol Access Point - we will exchange invoices over the Peppol network on your behalf.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Sending your invoices to your buyers** - ClearTax validates each invoice against European (EN 16931) and German national validation rules (including XRechnung and ZUGFeRD standards), and delivers it directly to your buyer's certified Peppol Access Point over the Peppol network. Outbound invoices are issued in standard Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 (UBL 2.1) or XRechnung syntax.

- ClearTax supports sending of all required document types under the German mandate (e.g., invoices, credit notes, debit notes).

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Receiving invoices from your suppliers** - Invoices arriving from your suppliers' Access Points over the Peppol network are accepted in mandate-compliant EN 16931 formats (primarily Peppol BIS 3.0 UBL and XRechnung), normalized into a common internal representation, and made available on the platform for your AP processing.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Lifecycle actions** - Your team can manage invoice statuses and communicate invoice responses (e.g., Acknowledgement, Acceptance, Rejection) directly with your counterparties using standard Peppol BIS Invoice Response 3.0 messages exchanged over the Peppol network.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Registration and authentication via VAT** - ClearTax registers your organisation's German VAT ID in the global Peppol Directory, allowing buyers and suppliers across Europe to discover your endpoint and route e-invoices to you automatically.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Self-Billed Invoicing** - Support for buyer-issued invoicing under formal self-billing agreements in compliance with German VAT legislation, using the Peppol BIS Self-Billing specification.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **API access** - REST endpoints for integrating with your ERP or accounting system: sending invoices, checking network delivery status, querying documents, processing invoice response messages, and managing data workflows.

### You can choose the integration method that fits your systems:

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Direct upload via the ClearTax web platform** - Upload invoice data via the ClearTax web interface.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **SFTP batch ingestion** - SFTP integration with standard CSV files for automated ingestion.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **REST API** - Direct REST API integration with your ERP or accounting system.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **RBAC** - Role-based access control lets your workspace administrator assign users to roles with different permissions, controlling who can view, create, approve, or act on invoices within your workspace, in line with your internal segregation-of-duties requirements.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Germany National & Peppol Business Scenarios** - ClearTax supports the standard range of business scenarios and syntax rules defined under OpenPEPPOL and German national extensions (including standard VAT treatments, reverse charges, zero-rated sales, intra-community transactions, and commercial discounts).

### 2. Onboarding

Onboarding covers the end-to-end setup needed for your business to start sending and receiving invoices through ClearTax as your certified Peppol Access Point (AP) under the German B2B mandate.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Business Hierarchy** - Your team can configure your business hierarchy on the platform based on the VAT ID and branches under a VAT as routing identifiers, ensuring invoices are correctly routed to the appropriate VAT or branch within your organisation.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Peppol Network Enrollment** - ClearTax enrolls your German VAT ID into the Peppol Directory under ClearTax's certified Access Point accreditation, enabling automated, secure message routing with all counterparty Peppol Access Points.

<span style={{color: '#22c55e'}}>✓</span> **Testing / Sandbox Environment** - A demo/sandbox environment is provided for your team and ERP integrations to test end-to-end invoice generation, validation, and Peppol transmission before going live in production.
