### 1. Tax Authority On-boarding

Two paths, both landing the customer on the ClearTax e-Invoicing dashboard:

1. **Via EmaraTax (FTA portal)** - customer logs in to EmaraTax, selects TIN, picks "Defmacro" (ClearTax) as ASP, gets redirected to ClearTax, verifies email/TIN, and a Peppol ID is generated and registered in the FTA directory.
2. **Via ClearTax** - customer signs up on ClearTax first, then is routed to EmaraTax to appoint Defmacro as ASP, and returns to ClearTax with TRN and org name prefilled.

Account setup (password, workspace, turnover band, profile) and business profile confirmation happen on ClearTax. Customer lands on the dashboard, ready to exchange invoices.

### 2. Off-boarding

1. Customer delinks ClearTax as ASP on EmaraTax
2. ClearTax deregisters the Peppol ID from the SMP
3. In-flight invoices and pending TDDs are drained
4. Archived documents remain accessible for the statutory retention period (or exported in bulk)
5. API keys, SFTP, and UI access are revoked

### 3. E-invoices supported

1. B2B
2. B2G
3. B2C _(out of scope for Phase 1)_

1. Tax Invoice
2. Credit Note
3. Debit Note

### 5. Channels

1. API
2. UI (manual / bulk upload)
3. SFTP

### 6. Accounts Receivable (ClearTax as C2 - seller side)

Customer sends eInvoices to buyers:

1. ClearTax ingests invoice data via API, UI, or SFTP
2. Validates data, enriches it, generates UUID
3. Looks up buyer's Access Point via Tickstar's SMP (SML)
4. Delivers the invoice to the buyer's AP over AS4
5. Captures buyer's MLS response and shows status to the seller

### 7. Accounts Payable (ClearTax as C3 - buyer side)

Customer receives eInvoices from suppliers:

1. Accepts inbound invoices from the seller's AP
2. Validates against PINT-AE schema and business rules
3. Sends MLS acknowledgement back to the seller's AP
4. Stores and shares the document via API, UI, and SFTP
5. Supports accept/reject and PO reconciliation

### 8. Government Reporting (FTA - via TDD)

1. Every exchange is reported to FTA as a TDD, on both seller and buyer sides, within the defined SLA
2. If the buyer sends MLS = RE (rejection), ClearTax withdraws the TDD from FTA and notifies the seller
3. TDS (value = F) is submitted on C2-side validation failure
4. Retry and resubmission of TDDs on failure or timeout

### 9. Statuses

**AR (Seller-side)**

- Pending - queued, awaiting submission
- Submitted to exchange network - handed off to Peppol
- Delivered to buyer network - reached buyer's AP (C3)
- Delivered to buyer - reached buyer's ERP (C4)
- Failed - validation or delivery failed

**AP (Buyer-side)**

- Received - invoice arrived from supplier
- Failed - validation failed on receipt

**Government Reporting (FTA)**

- Not Submitted - TDD not yet dispatched
- Processing - TDD sent, awaiting response
- Accepted - FTA accepted the TDD
- Withdrawn - TDD withdrawn (following buyer RE)

**MLS response codes**

- AP - Accepted
- AB - Accepted with objection
- RE - Rejected (with reason code: SV syntax / BV business rule / BW warning / FD fraud)
