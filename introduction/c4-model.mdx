The 4-corner model is a decentralised e-invoicing framework that facilitates the exchange of structured invoices between suppliers and buyers through certified intermediaries known as Access Points, without a tax authority as a direct party to the exchange. It standardises communication protocols and data formats, letting businesses transact with any number of trading partners through a single connection instead of maintaining separate direct connections with each one.

## The four corners

- **Corner 1 - Supplier (sender)** - initiates the transaction by producing the structured invoice; accuracy here is critical since it drives all downstream automation.
- **Corner 2 - Supplier's Access Point (e.g. ClearTax)** - the supplier's certified gateway to the network; authenticates, validates, and forwards the invoice to the correct destination.
- **Corner 3 - Buyer's Access Point** - the buyer's certified representative; verifies the invoice, enforces compliance checks, and makes it compatible with the buyer's systems.
- **Corner 4 - Buyer (receiver)** - accepts the invoice and processes it internally, including validation, purchase-order reconciliation, and payment.

## How the transaction flows

1. **Invoice generation** - the supplier creates the invoice in the prescribed electronic format.
2. **Validation and transmission** - the supplier's Access Point confirms the structure and sends it via secure channels.
3. **Reception and verification** - the buyer's Access Point receives and validates the document.
4. **Buyer acceptance** - the buyer electronically accepts the invoice.

## Why it matters

- **Regulatory compliance** - aligns structured invoice exchange with national and regional standards as more markets move toward mandatory e-invoicing.
- **Simplified integration landscape** - replaces the need for separate connections per trading partner with a single connection that supports multiple relationships.
- **Operational efficiency** - automates the invoicing lifecycle from validation to posting, minimising delays and manual intervention.
- **Cost optimisation** - reduces the overhead of maintaining multiple integrations and manual processes by standardising communication.
- **Enhanced auditability** - every transaction is recorded and traceable across the network, easing audits and long-term record-keeping.
- **ERP and system impact** - connects directly with ERP systems for real-time invoice processing, more accurate reporting, and faster reconciliation.

## How it compares

- **vs. the 5-corner (C5) model** - the 4-corner model links businesses through Access Points only; the 5-corner model adds the tax authority as a fifth corner for real-time reporting and compliance monitoring.
