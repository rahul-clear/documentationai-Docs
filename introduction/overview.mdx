Welcome to ClearTax's E-Invoicing documentation. This guide covers compliance requirements, integration flows, and API references for e-invoicing across supported countries.

## E-invoicing models

ClearTax's e-invoicing integrations are built on three underlying regulatory models. Every country mandate maps to one of these:

- **[C5 Model](/introduction/c5-model)** - a decentralised 5-corner framework where the tax authority is looped in for real-time reporting alongside the supplier and buyer Access Points. Live in UAE, Oman, and France.
- **[C4 Model](/introduction/c4-model)** - a decentralised 4-corner framework where structured invoices are exchanged between suppliers and buyers through certified Access Points, without the tax authority as a direct party. Live in Belgium, Germany, and Croatia.
- **[Clearance + Reporting Model](/introduction/clearance-reporting-model)** - a centralised framework where the seller's solution submits invoices directly to the tax authority's platform, which either clears the invoice before it reaches the buyer or receives it for reporting shortly after issuance. Live in KSA and Jordan.

Pick the model that matches your country to understand how invoices flow between you, ClearTax, and the tax authority before diving into that country's setup and API reference.
